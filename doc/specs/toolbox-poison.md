## 1. Purpose and scope

The "Poison" section of the Gameplay Toolbox (SRD 5.2.1, pp. 197-198):

- the four **types** of poison (Contact, Ingested, Inhaled, Injury) and how each is **delivered**: smeared on an object, swallowed, released as a 5-foot Cube, applied as a Bonus Action to a weapon or ammunition and delivered by Piercing or Slashing damage; partial doses of Ingested poison;
- **Harvesting Poison**: the time, the DC 20 Intelligence (Nature) check with a Poisoner's Kit, one dose per creature, exposure on a failure by 5 or more;
- the **fourteen sample poisons** (Assassin's Blood to Wyvern Poison) as data: price, type and a `SaveGatedEffect` each, plus the three things the hazard model lists as gaps for this spec: the early end of Unconscious ("wakes up if it takes damage"), the riders that are not conditions (Speed halved, cannot knowingly communicate a lie) and the Midnight Tears neutralisation trigger.

**What this spec does not own.** The save-then-effect mechanism (save, margin of failure, damage and condition consequences, half damage on a success, repeat saves and their success count, rolled durations, midnight onset, ending and neutralising, unhealable damage) is owned by [toolbox-hazard-effects.md](toolbox-hazard-effects.md); siblings "never define a second effect model". Section 3.1 maps each poison concept to the hazard type that carries it. A poison is a thin `PoisonDefinition` around a `SaveGatedEffect`, and the use cases delegate to the hazard domain services. A hazard gap is requested by amending the hazard spec, never defined in parallel.

**Where the data lives (shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-045, FR-047).** The fourteen samples are named catalogue entries, so they are a `content` kind (`poison`, revision row R3-poisons, content-schema-and-decoder.md FR-129), not package-level tables in `toolbox`. This spec owns the entry shape (`content.PoisonEntry`, section 3.3); content-schema-and-decoder.md owns the envelope, `content.PoisonID` and the decoder. `toolbox` converts a decoded entry into a `PoisonDefinition` (FR-083). The harvest check constants stay unexported constants in package `toolbox`.

**Supersedes (shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-048).** No requirement row of an older spec. It resolves the pointers older specs left to it: the "`toolbox-poison`" gap rows of toolbox-hazard-effects.md section 5.1, the removed `PoisonSaveRequest`/`PoisonTestOutcome`/`ResolvePoisonSave` mapping in its section 5.2, content-schema-and-decoder.md FR-129 / R3-poisons / Q16, and toolbox-traps.md Q-18. It keeps `PoisonAffliction` as a wrapper around `ActiveSaveGatedEffect` (poison facts the hazard state lacks: the Poisoned hold, woken kinds, midnight, unhealable damage). It does not take Basic Poison or Antitoxin (Q-19).

The engine follows the package default, state in, new state and events out (`doc/rules/architecture.md` §4). **Delivery** (`ExposeToPoison`) decides whether a dose reaches a creature and which first-save modifiers a partial dose carries; the host then resolves the save with the hazard use cases. Poison use cases **return values and never apply them** (architecture-guardrails.md FR-097; toolbox-hazard-effects.md FR-046): damage goes to `ApplyDamage` with the returned adjustments; conditions come back as `ConditionKind` lists the host applies with `ApplyConditionToSet` and `RemoveConditionFromSet`. The host decides **when** things happen; the engine reads no clock.

**Out of scope:**

- **Purchasing Poison** (p. 197): laws, illicit apothecaries, bribes. GM advice with no rule; the suggested price per dose is data (FR-017).
- **Basic Poison and Antitoxin** (SRD 5.2.1, p. 99 and pp. 95-96). Owned by [item-use-effects.md](item-use-effects.md): its own `BasicPoisonCoating` (no save, no `PoisonType`; `PoisonCoating` cannot be reused across the combat/toolbox edge) and `AntitoxinEffect`, with `PoisonedSaveIntent` derived by the host from `poisonedAtStake` (FR-019), a second consumer beside Dwarven Resilience. Neither is a "Sample Poison" (Q-19).
- **Monster poisons.** The poison "of a venomous creature" harvested under "Harvesting Poison" is whatever the stat block says (`content-monsters.md`); which poison applies on a harvest failure by 5 or more is the host's decision (Q-12).
- **The Poisoned condition** (Disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks, p. 186), the Petrified immunity to it (`conditions.md` FR-023, FR-084, FR-085) and **damage handling** (Resistance and Immunity to Poison damage, Temporary Hit Points, Healing: `hit-points-and-damage.md`). The one new fact, damage that "can't be healed" (Pale Tincture), is the hazard flag `SaveGatedDamageHealing` (toolbox-hazard-effects.md FR-045); the healing ceiling is an interpretation (FR-047, Q-11).
- **Resolving saves and checks.** The Constitution save and the Intelligence (Nature) check are D20 Tests resolved by `ResolveD20Test`; the caller builds the modifiers (Constitution modifier, Proficiency Bonus with the Poisoner's Kit, Advantage when also proficient in Nature, "Tool Proficiency", p. 93; Dwarven Resilience's and Antitoxin's Advantage) and passes the finished result in.
- **Which creatures are in the 5-foot Cube** and any geometry (p. 197; a fact of the Inhaled type, not an exported constant). The host calls `ExposeToPoison` once per creature and shares one damage roll (FR-078, FR-079).
- **Spending the Bonus Action** to coat an Injury poison (FR-010): `initiative-and-actions.md`; this spec's Q-13. **Time keeping:** durations are data; the engine never expires an effect (toolbox-hazard-effects.md FR-054); the host calls `EndSaveGatedEffect` (FR-064 there; FR-080 here).
- **Poisons in traps** (Poisoned Darts, Poisoned Needle, pp. 200-201): the trap spec. **Spells that detect, neutralise or protect against poison** (Protection from Poison, Detect Poison and Disease): spell content; a spell that ends Poisoned is how Midnight Tears is neutralised (FR-050).
- **Rules from outside SRD 5.2.1:** no named-monster poisons, other editions' poisons or craft rules.

### 3.3 The `poison` content kind (owned here, registered as R3-poisons)

`PoisonEntry` is a plain data record in package `content`, decoded from the `poisons` array. It uses only upstream vocabulary (`ability.Ability`, `dice.Expression`, `DamageType`, `ConditionKind`, and `DurationUnit` for the nested units, owned by content-schema-and-decoder.md, shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-065; Q-22 (6)) and no `toolbox` type, plus the optional envelope `source` (`SourceCitation`, content-schema-and-decoder.md FR-034; mandatory for bundled data by FR-090). Its id is `PoisonID`, a comparable string-backed value owned by content-schema-and-decoder.md FR-114, `snake_case` per shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-007. JSON field names are camelCase; the decoder rejects unknown keys and reports all problems at once as an `errors.Join` of typed problems.

| Field | Type | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `id`, `name` | `PoisonID`, string | Printed name, lower-cased, apostrophes removed (table 4.11a). |
| `priceGp` | int, 0 or more | "suggested price for a single dose" (p. 197). |
| `type` | `PoisonType` | The four types of p. 197. |
| `saveAbility`, `saveDc` | `Ability`, int (1 or more) | Constitution and the printed DC for all fourteen. |
| `onset` | `immediate` or `atMidnight` | Midnight Tears only. |
| `onFailure` | damage list (`dice.Expression`, `DamageType`, optional printed average) and condition list (`ConditionKind`, optional `failedByAtLeast`) | Failed-save outcome. |
| `onSuccess` | `none` or `halfDamage` | "takes half as much damage only" (p. 197). |
| `repeat` | absent, or trigger (`turnStart`, `turnEnd`, `afterInterval` with amount and unit), `successesToEnd`, failure (`noFurtherEffect` or `reapplyDamage` list) | Repeat saves; `successesToEnd` maps to `SaveGatedRepeatSuccessCounts` with `SaveGatedEndsAfterSuccesses(successesToEnd)` (FR-083). |
| `duration` | `instantaneous`, `untilEnded`, `fixed` (amount, unit) or `rolled` (`dice.Expression`, unit) | "for 24 hours", "4d6 hours", "until the poison ends". |
| `healing` | `healable` or `unhealableWhileEffectLasts` | Pale Tincture (p. 198). |
| `riders` | list of `speedHalved`, `cannotKnowinglyLie` | Non-condition riders. |
| `wakeRules` | list of (`ConditionKind`, `onDamage` or `onDamageOrShaken`) | Early end of one condition. |
| `neutralization` | `none` or `anyEffectEndingPoisoned` | Midnight Tears. |

Results follow shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-017 and FR-019: each use case returns its own closed interface `<UseCase>Result` (`ExposeToPoisonResult`, `ProgressPoisonResult`, `HarvestPoisonResult`) with operation-prefixed variants; events are past-tense values of the closed interface `PoisonEvent` family.

## 5. Model

Context `toolbox`, one package (`toolbox/`) with the poison files `poison_*.go` and `*_usecase.go` for the use cases. Value types are immutable (unexported fields, validating constructors, value receivers, no setters) and pure and synchronous. Result and event naming follows shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-017 and FR-019; every exported type is prefixed (FR-075).

- **Referenced, not defined:** every type of section 3.1, plus `ability.Ability`, `modifier.RollCircumstance` (`Advantage`), `dice.Expression` (the kernel packages); `DifficultyClass`, `CheckResult`, `ForcedFailureResult`, `Kind` (`d20`); `ConditionKind`, `DamageType`, `Skill`, `PoisonID` (`content`); `Damage`, `DamageAdjustment`, `CombatantID` (`combat`).
- **Content side (package `content`):** `PoisonEntry` and `PoisonType` (section 3.3), data-only and free of any `toolbox` type. `PoisonID` is content-schema-and-decoder.md FR-114.
- **`PoisonDefinition`** (`poison_definition.go`): fields in section 3.2; `Riders` and `WakeRules` return copies. `NewPoisonDefinition` enforces FR-016 and nothing the hazard constructors already enforce, and returns an error wrapping `srderr.ErrInvalidInput` otherwise (`MustPoisonDefinition` panics). `NewPoisonDefinition(entry)` builds it from a decoded entry (FR-083). Use cases take a definition in the command; the host fetches the entry from a `content.Source`.
- **`PoisonAffliction`** (no identity; the host holds one per creature and poison): `Definition`, `Effect` (replaced after every hazard call by `WithEffect`), `PoisonedHold`, `EndedWakeKinds` (a copy), `Midnight`, `UnhealableDamage` (int, 0 or more). It stores no stage, schedule, duration, success count or end reason: those are on `Effect` (toolbox-hazard-effects.md FR-021 to FR-023, FR-054, FR-063). Invariants: counts never negative; an ended affliction contributes nothing to `PoisonEffects` and `PoisonEnding`; `Midnight` is `reached` only while the phase is `SaveGatedPhaseAwaitingOnset`. Operations: `StartPoisonAffliction(definition, effect)`, `WithEffect`, `WithPoisonedRefused()` (FR-033). The zero value is invalid (`IsZero`).
- **Delivery:** `PoisonCoating` (`Poison`, `Type`, `Object`, `State`; operations `ApplyPoisonCoating` (function), `WashOff`, unexported `spend`); `PoisonDelivery` is a closed interface (unexported marker method) with exactly the variants `PoisonContactDelivery{Coating, SkinContact}`, `PoisonIngestedDelivery{Dose}`, `PoisonInhaledDelivery` and `PoisonInjuryDelivery{Coating, DamageType, DamageTaken}`.
- **Queries** (stateless functions): `PoisonEffects.Speed`, `Communication`, `HealingCeiling` (FR-036, FR-037, FR-047); `PoisonEnding.ConditionsToRemove` (FR-080); `PoisonedAtStake` (FR-019). They take the `[]PoisonAffliction` of one creature and count only afflictions in `SaveGatedPhaseActive`.
- **Rule services** (stateless, pure, synchronous, in unexported types of package `toolbox`; FR-087):
  - `poisonDeliveryRules.evaluate(definition, delivery, sharedDamage)` returns an unexported closed-interface `poisonDeliveryDecision` (exposed, not delivered with a reason, rejected with a reason). Order: variant against `PoisonType` (FR-015), the shared damage list (FR-078), then FR-002 to FR-013.
  - `poisonProgressRules.apply(affliction, progress, otherAfflictions)` returns an unexported closed-interface `poisonProgressDecision` (updated, ended, rider ended, no change, rejected): FR-046, FR-048 to FR-052, FR-055 to FR-057, FR-059, FR-086 and the FR-052 table. It ends an effect by calling the exported hazard `SaveGatedEvaluator` ending rules (toolbox-hazard-effects.md FR-064 to FR-067).
  - `harvestPoisonRules.reasons(target, availability, kit, check)` returns the rejection reasons in order (FR-061, FR-062, FR-077; empty means accepted and the use case may roll); `harvestPoisonRules.resolve(check, availability, minutesRolled)` returns the outcome, availability and events (FR-064 to FR-069).
- **Events:** the closed interface `PoisonEvent` family embedding `ToolboxEvent` (FR-074): `PoisonRiderEnded{Target, PoisonID, Kind}`, `PoisonDoseHarvested{MinutesSpent, Doses}`, `PoisonHarvestAttemptFailed{MinutesSpent}`, `PoisonHarvesterExposed{MinutesSpent}`. Hazard events come from the hazard use cases and pass through unchanged when `ProgressPoison` ends an effect.
- **Failures.** Expected outcomes are closed-interface results (every `...NotDelivered`, `...Rejected`, `...NoChange` variant). Host-supplied values that fail validation (a delivery for the wrong type, a shared damage list that does not fit, a harvest check of the wrong kind or DC, an `otherAfflictions` entry of another creature) are closed-interface rejections (`doc/rules/architecture.md` §6; toolbox-hazard-effects.md FR-039, FR-042, FR-078), never errors or panics. A violated precondition (an invalid definition, a negative loss or damage taken) is an error wrapping `srderr.ErrInvalidInput` from the constructor (`MustX` panics). The only port failure is the dice port's, returned wrapped once as `*srderr.PortError` (FR-072).

## 6. Interface

All use cases are synchronous: their only port, `Roller`, is synchronous (`doc/rules/architecture.md` §4). Each is built with `NewX(...opts)` (`WithTelemetry`, default `telemetry.Noop`; `WithClock`; a nil `Roller` is an error) and has one method `Run(ctx context.Context, cmd <UseCase>Command) (<UseCase>Result, error)`, where `<UseCase>Result` is its own closed interface with operation-prefixed variants (shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-017) and the `error` carries only a port failure or a cancelled `ctx`. `ctx` is the first parameter because every use case emits spans. **No poison use case calls another use case** (FR-044 there): `ProgressPoison` ends an effect with the hazard domain evaluator, not its use case. Each use case only orchestrates (FR-087) and records only its own signals.

| Use case | Command | Result variants | Port |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ExposeToPoison` | `definition`, `delivery`, optional `sharedDamage` (`[]Damage`) | `ExposeToPoisonExposed(effect, delivery, firstSaveCircumstances, firstSaveDamageAdjustments, damageAmount)`, `ExposeToPoisonNotDelivered(reason, delivery)`, `ExposeToPoisonRejected(reason)` | none |
| `ProgressPoison` | `affliction`, `progress` (`PoisonProgress`), `otherAfflictions` (default empty); the target is `affliction.effect.target` (FR-086) | `ProgressPoisonUpdated(affliction)`, `ProgressPoisonEnded(affliction, conditionsToRemove, events)`, `ProgressPoisonRiderEnded(affliction, conditionsToRemove, events)`, `ProgressPoisonNoChange(affliction)`, `ProgressPoisonRejected(reason)` | none |
| `HarvestPoison` | `target` (`HarvestPoisonTarget(venom, state)`; `state` is dead or alive with an `incapacitated` flag), `availability`, `kit`, `check` | `HarvestPoisonCompleted(outcome, minutesSpent, availability, events)`, `HarvestPoisonRejected(reasons)` | `Roller` |

- **Host protocol** (in `toolbox/doc.go`). To expose a creature: call `ExposeToPoison`; on `Exposed`, call the hazard `ResolveSaveGatedEffect` with `SaveGatedInitialExposure(effect, target)`, the creature's `SaveGatedSubject`, the save (`SaveGatedSaveNotRolled` first, then the finished `SaveGatedSaveRolled` or `SaveGatedSaveForcedFailure`) and the returned `damageAmount`; add `firstSaveCircumstances` to that save only; apply the `consequences` with the `combat` damage function (hazard adjustments, plus `firstSaveDamageAdjustments` when that save failed) and the condition-apply function; wrap the returned `active` with `StartPoisonAffliction`; report `ConditionImmune` with `WithPoisonedRefused()`; call `ResolveRepeatSave` on each trigger and `EndSaveGatedEffect` when a duration elapses; call `ProgressPoison` for midnight, damage, shaking, an effect that ended Poisoned, and every Hit Point lost to unhealable damage; remove the returned `conditionsToRemove` with the condition-remove function, treating a kind not held as already cleared (FR-081). For an area call `RollSaveGatedDamage` once and pass the result as `sharedDamage` to every target (FR-079).
- Reason enums are in section 3.2. A delivery that does not match the poison type is a returned rejection (FR-015); an `ErrInvalidInput` error is kept for true preconditions (a negative `damageTaken` or `hitPointsLost`, an invalid definition). `HarvestPoisonRejected.Reasons`, `conditionsToRemove` (first-granted order) and `events` are copies. Domain operations the host may call directly: `ApplyPoisonCoating` and `PoisonCoating.WashOff`, `StartPoisonAffliction`, `WithEffect` and `WithPoisonedRefused`, `PoisonEffects.Speed`, `Communication` and `HealingCeiling`, `PoisonEnding.ConditionsToRemove`, `PoisonedAtStake`, `PoisonHarvestCheck`.
- **Ports:** only `Roller` (a consumer-side interface satisfied by `dice.Roller`). No new port, adapter or test double (shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-037, FR-038), so no contract suite. Failure contract: a dice port failure is the returned `*srderr.PortError`; `dice.Roll` rejects a wrong result (errors-and-results.md FR-036).
- **Exports.** Package `toolbox` exports the three use cases with commands and results and all variants, and every type of section 3.2 except `PoisonType`, `PoisonID` and `PoisonEntry` (exported by `content`, content-schema-and-decoder.md FR-114), plus `PoisonEffects`, `PoisonEnding` and the `PoisonEvent` family. It does not export any unprefixed name (FR-075). `toolbox/doc.go` lists inputs, outputs and the host protocol. The sample catalogue is data (FR-085), not a toolbox export.
- **Dependencies.** the kernel packages (`ability`, `dice`, `modifier`, `srderr`, `telemetry`), `content` (`ConditionKind`, `DamageType`, `Skill`, `ItemID`, `PoisonEntry`, `PoisonType`, `PoisonID`), `d20` (`CheckResult`, `ForcedFailureResult`, `DifficultyClass`, `Kind`) and `combat` (`CombatantID`, `Damage`, `DamageAdjustment`): published-language values or ids of upstream contexts (architecture-guardrails.md FR-097, FR-018, FR-019). **Not used:** `ConditionSet`, `ConditionEffects`, `ApplyConditionToSetResult`, `RemoveConditionFromSetResult`, `ConditionApplied`, `ConditionRemoved` (the host owns every condition application, toolbox-hazard-effects.md FR-046); nothing from `character`, nothing downstream.

## 7. Observability

The domain (every non-`*_usecase.go` poison file) is **silent by design** (`doc/rules/observability.md` §3); only the three use cases are instrumented. The hazard spec owns `srd5e.toolbox.operations`, `operation_duration` and `errors` for the whole context; this spec lists its own operation and result values and adds its constants to the signal-name constants itself (FR-088; section 5), defining no second signals file. No log events: spans and metrics carry everything (`doc/rules/observability.md` §7).

**Operations and results (Q-22).** This spec's `srd5e.toolbox.operation` values are `expose_to_poison`, `progress_poison` and `harvest_poison`; their `srd5e.toolbox.result` values, with the shared `srd5e.outcome` (shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-041; `doc/rules/observability.md` §5):

| Use case | `srd5e.toolbox.result` values | `srd5e.outcome` |
|---|---|---|
| `expose_to_poison` | `exposed`, `not_delivered` | `success` |
| `expose_to_poison` | `rejected_delivery_does_not_match_poison`, `rejected_shared_damage_mismatch` | `failure` |
| `progress_poison` | `updated`, `ended`, `rider_ended`, `no_change` | `success` |
| `progress_poison` | `rejected_affliction_ended`, `rejected_progress_not_applicable`, `rejected_other_affliction_of_another_creature` | `failure` |
| `harvest_poison` | `succeeded`, `failed`, `failed_exposed` | `success` |
| `harvest_poison` | `rejected` (one value however many reasons are listed) | `failure` |
| `harvest_poison` | `error` (a dice port error left the call, toolbox-hazard-effects.md FR-078) | `failure` |

A rejection sets `srd5e.outcome` `failure` and the span status to error, counted once, with no `error.type` and no log (toolbox-hazard-effects.md FR-073; `doc/rules/observability.md` §6; only a returned dice port failure carries `error.type`; Q-23). A `harvest_poison` call ending in a dice port failure still increments `srd5e.toolbox.operations` once (outcome `failure`, result `error`) and records `operation_duration` before the wrapped error is returned (toolbox-hazard-effects.md FR-078, FR-081). The other two use cases roll no dice and have no `error` result. A `not_delivered` result and a failed harvest are normal rule outcomes and are `success`.

Signals this spec owns (hazard signals are reused, not repeated):

| Signal | Type | Attributes | Purpose | Suggested monitoring |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `srd5e.toolbox.expose_to_poison` | span | `srd5e.outcome`, `srd5e.toolbox.result`, `srd5e.toolbox.poison.type` | One `ExposeToPoison` call | Trace sampling |
| `srd5e.toolbox.progress_poison`, `srd5e.toolbox.harvest_poison` | span | `srd5e.outcome`, `srd5e.toolbox.result` | One call of each | Trace sampling |
| `srd5e.toolbox.poison.exposures` | counter, `{exposure}` | `srd5e.toolbox.poison.type` (`contact`, `ingested`, `inhaled`, `injury`), `srd5e.toolbox.poison.delivery_result` (`exposed`, `skin_covered`, `partial_dose_no_effect`, `not_piercing_or_slashing`, `no_damage_taken`, `coating_spent`) | How often a dose reaches a creature and why not (every delivery decision; a rejection is counted only in `operations`) | Dashboard by type and result. When `exposed` is under 50% for one type over 1 hour with at least 100 calls, check the host's delivery wiring (for example a `damageTaken` of 0 passed for every hit). |

Shared signals reused with added values (`srd5e.toolbox.operations`, `operation_duration` for every outcome including rejections and port failures, `srd5e.toolbox.errors` with operation `harvest_poison` and `error.type` `dice_roller_error`): thresholds are the hazard's (rejected_* above 5% of an operation over 1 hour; p99 above twice the 7-day baseline, where a `harvest_poison` regression means a slow `Roller` adapter; any `errors` over 5 minutes). Attribute values are lowercase `snake_case`. Poison ids are not attributes (unbounded); the type is bounded at four values.

The seven evaluation questions (`doc/rules/observability.md` §2): (1) what can go wrong: the dice port fails or answers for another expression (harvest only); out-of-order calls; a dose that never lands; the host forgetting Pale Tincture damage reports or the hazard repeat and ending calls; a wrong-type delivery, shared damage list or harvest check (returned rejections). (2) How an operator knows: span error and `srd5e.toolbox.errors` for port failure; `failure` outcomes on `operations` for out-of-order calls; the `poison.exposures` result mix for doses that never land; the hazard `ended` count stalling while exposures continue. (3) Diagnosis: the result carries the effect, affliction, kinds to remove and events; the span carries type and result. (4) Counted or timed: operations by result, exposures by type and delivery result, latency, errors by `error.type`. (5) Existing instrumentation is still correct: only operation and result values are added (FR-088). (6) Consumers monitor the thresholds above. (7) Nothing is noisy: no logs, spans only on the three use cases.

Every signal feeds `doc/observability/signals.md` in the same change as the code (`doc/rules/observability.md` §2).

## 9. Open questions and decisions

No question is open and none is escalated. Each is settled by SRD text (cited) or package design; "interpretation" marks a reading the SRD does not state. Common community rulings agree with these readings and are not used as a source. Settled, one line each:
- **Q-1** this spec sits on top of the hazard model and asks for hazard changes only by amendment (Q-22). **Q-2, Q-3** the catalogue is a content kind and `toolbox` is the downstream context (shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-045, FR-047). **Q-6** a partial-dose ruling is a per-delivery value, never a global option; the set is closed to the SRD's two examples plus `PoisonNoReducedEffect`. **Q-14** domain code returns `ConditionKind` lists, no condition-set type (architecture-guardrails.md FR-097; toolbox-hazard-effects.md FR-046). **Q-15** (damage rolled once for several targets) is FR-078 and FR-079, id not reused. The printed average is a per-call input, not banned (FR-027).
- **Q-4** `class-rogue.md`'s Cunning Strike Poison types stay separate from `SaveGatedEffect`: `character` cannot import `toolbox`, and a shared type would have to live upstream of both, which no current need justifies (toolbox-hazard-effects.md Q-4).
- **Q-5** (interpretation) a successful first save means no effect and no repeats; only repeat saves count; successes are cumulative because the SRD says "three successful saves" and never "consecutive" (pp. 197-198; FR-029, FR-044; toolbox-hazard-effects.md FR-057).
- **Q-7** (interpretation) a forced failure of a harvest check has no total, so it is a failure by less than 5 (`HarvestPoisonFailed`, no exposure). The SRD names no margin for an automatic failure; the milder reading is chosen because exposure needs an actual shortfall of 5 (p. 197). The host may call `ExposeToPoison` itself if its table rules otherwise (FR-069).
- **Q-8** (interpretation) a creature immune to Poisoned is reported with `withPoisonedRefused()` and the affliction holds and grants nothing (FR-033); an already Poisoned creature keeps the affliction active (FR-034); on ending, Poisoned is offered for removal only when no other active affliction holds it, since `conditions.md` stores one condition per kind without sources (FR-080; "Condition", p. 179).
- **Q-9** (interpretation) Midnight Tears is neutralised before midnight by any effect ending Poisoned whatever the creature's conditions (FR-050, p. 198); a poison that holds Poisoned ends with it (FR-052, "while Poisoned in this way", p. 197); one that never held it (Burnt Othur Fumes) is unaffected. The host reports the trigger.
- **Q-10** (interpretation) Torpor halves the Speed `ConditionEffects.speed` returns, rounded down (p. 187), once however many doses are active; Speed 0 stays 0 (FR-036).
- **Q-11** (interpretation) Pale Tincture's unhealable damage is tracked per affliction and `PoisonEffects.healingCeiling` (FR-047) is the poison-owned formula; the host passes it as the optional healing ceiling of `RestoreHitPoints` (hit-points-and-damage.md FR-115, `HealingCeilingAt`), which stays unaware of poison. Closes toolbox-hazard-effects.md Q-7.
- **Q-12** "venomous" is not a SRD flag, so the host passes `HarvestPoisonVenomPresence`; the host applies the monster's poison with `ExposeToPoison`; a failed attempt may be repeated because the SRD bars further harvesting only after a success (FR-067, FR-068; p. 197).
- **Q-13** the Bonus Action cost of coating is reported as data (FR-010); enforcing it is the host's action-economy concern, and `BonusActionGrant` (a feature grant, `initiative-and-actions.md` FR-060) is not stretched to cover an item use. No amendment.
- **Q-16** (interpretation) any touch spends a Contact coating; only exposed skin exposes the creature (FR-003; "until it is touched or washed off", p. 197).
- **Q-17** (interpretation) Ingested and Inhaled poison cannot be coated: the SRD describes coating only for Contact and Injury poison (FR-011, p. 197).
- **Q-18** (interpretation for the 0 case only) exposed when `DamageTaken.total` is at least 1, Temporary Hit Points counting as damage taken; damage reduced to 0 does not expose (FR-012, p. 197).
- **Q-19** Basic Poison (p. 99) and Antitoxin (pp. 95-96) are owned by item-use-effects.md (its own `BasicPoisonCoating`; the host derives `PoisonedSaveIntent` from `poisonedAtStake`, FR-019); not this spec and not Sample Poisons.
- **Q-20** a partial dose ruling "dealing only half as much damage on a failed save" (p. 197) applies on a failed first save only. A successful save keeps the poison's own `HalfDamage`, so a repeated `HalfDamage` on one `Damage` never occurs and `hit-points-and-damage.md` FR-099 needs no change (FR-007, table 4.12a).
- **Q-21** (interpretation) a partial dose's ruling applies to the first save and its damage only; repeat saves are the poison's own progression and the Midnight Tears delayed save is the first save (FR-076). A table ruling otherwise changes the host's input, not the engine.
- **Q-22** hazard amendments are all applied: operations, results (including `error` for `harvest_poison`) and the poison keys are listed in section 7 and added to the `toolbox` signal constants by this spec (FR-088); the effect is built and evaluated through `SaveGatedRequirement`, `SaveGatedRepeatSaves` and the `SaveGatedEvaluator` operations `EvaluateInitial`, `EvaluateRepeat`, `EvaluateEnd` (hazard section 5), which the use cases reach through the hazard use cases. This spec edits no other file.
- **Q-23** rejections for host-protocol misuse follow toolbox-hazard-effects.md FR-073 (errors-and-results.md FR-043): a returned `...Rejected` is outcome `failure` with error span status, no `error.type`, no log, while `not_delivered` and a failed harvest are `success` (FR-073, section 7).
