> Go notation used below. A name in lowerCamelCase (a field, command argument or enum member) stands for the exported PascalCase Go identifier (`attackerID` is the field `AttackerID`). A trailing `?` marks an optional value (the zero value or a `(T, bool)` pair). `[]T` is a slice; `set of T` is a collection without duplicates. A `(T, error)` pair replaces an accepted/rejected result pair: the nil-error case is the accepted case and the error is the typed rejection, satisfying `errors.Is(err, srderr.ErrInvalidInput)`; a parser reports every problem at once through `errors.Join`. Rule outcomes (refusals, not-possible stops) stay result values. A use case is a struct with `Run(ctx, cmd)`; its `error` is non-nil only for a port failure.

## 1. Purpose and scope

Specifies the Warlock class as SRD 5.2.1 presents it (pp. 70-76): the Core Warlock Traits, the Warlock Features table, every class feature from level 1 to 20 (Eldritch Invocations, Pact Magic, Magical Cunning, Contact Patron, Mystic Arcanum, Eldritch Master), the Eldritch Invocation Options, the Warlock spell list, and the one subclass the SRD gives, Fiend Patron. Each feature has two parts:

1. **Content data**, owned by `content-classes.md` (shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-025): the Warlock `ClassDefinition`, the Warlock Features table as `ProgressionRow`s, Fiend Patron as a `SubclassDefinition` (its Fiend Spells are `AlwaysPreparedSpells` facts, Table FA) and the invocation options as `FeatureOption`s (Table OP, FR-061, FR-062). The spell list is derived from `Spell.Classes` (content-spells.md). This spec defines none of these and keeps no second copy. The one input with no data home is the cantrip classification (damage, attack roll, range), owned here as the pure-domain input `CantripFacts` (FR-156).
2. **Behaviour in the `character` context** (this spec owns behaviour only): pure domain functions from (character state, inputs) to (new state, events) for every feature, plus one use case for each feature that needs dice or content through a port (decisions FR-023). Warlock state is a set of `FeatureState` values in the character's `FeatureStates` under typed keys defined here (decisions FR-022). Spell preparation, cantrip choice and Pact Magic slots are spellcasting.md's; this spec adds only the Warlock-specific interactions.

Features resolved in `combat` (the push of Repelling Blast, the Prone condition of Eldritch Smite, Temporary Hit Points, Resistance) are exposed here only as plain values and content vocabulary types (`DamageType`, `ConditionKind`, `CreatureSize`); applying them is `combat`'s job. `character` never imports `combat`, and this spec depends on no `combat` spec and no other `class-*.md` (decisions FR-013, FR-016).

**Out of scope:**

- General spellcasting rules and everything the Warlock page says about Pact Magic mechanics: the slot table (spellcasting.md FR-012), cantrips known (FR-014), prepared spells (FR-015, FR-016, FR-041, FR-042), replacement (FR-048, FR-049, FR-053), the Arcane Focus (FR-068), the Short Rest restore (FR-026) and Concentration (decisions FR-011). This spec states only the Warlock-specific interactions: the always-prepared spells of Fiend Spells and Contact Other Plane, the arcanum rules, the Pact of the Tome book and the Eldritch Mind Advantage source.
- Everything the decisions move out of class specs: the `ClassDefinition`, `SubclassDefinition`, `ProgressionRow` and bulk class data and the decoder checks (content-classes.md, content-schema-and-decoder.md); `Subclass` with a nil error, an error, subclass progression, feat grants and multiclass trait gains (level-advancement-and-multiclassing.md, feats-behaviour.md); `RestKind` and `RestApplied` (rests-and-hit-dice.md). Their ids are in "Withdrawn ids".
- The definitions of the spells named here and of Ritual, school and Concentration (content-spells.md); this spec stores `SpellID`s only. The two spell facts the invocation Prerequisites need and the Eldritch Spear range test are `CantripFacts` (table 4.6.8, FR-156, FR-157). The die of False Life is not needed: Fiendish Vigor gives the maximum, resolved where the spell is.
- Items of Starting Equipment, armor, weapons and properties (equipment content specs; this spec keeps names and quantities as `EquipmentLine`s, content-classes.md FR-025, and leaves `ItemID` resolution to character-creation.md; the Pact of the Blade weapon is looked up by `ItemID` through the content port); the stat blocks of the familiar forms (content-monsters.md, by `MonsterID`, FR-090; a normal form is a Beast of Challenge Rating 0 checked on its monster entry, FR-090); the Ability Score Improvement and Epic Boon feats and Origin feats (this spec records only the Origin feat choices of Lessons of the First Ones, FR-104).
- Rules for rests, Hit Point Dice, Temporary Hit Points, Resistance, damage types, conditions, senses, Speeds, actions, attunement, Extra Attack and planes (`combat` or the general rules specs), and converting "1 minute" or "until the end of your next turn" to rounds (the domain has no clock; elapsed time arrives from the host, FR-106, FR-085).
- Other subclasses (the SRD gives only Fiend Patron, p. 76). No Warlock material outside SRD 5.2.1 pp. 70-76 was used.

## 5. Model

### 5.1 Where each type lives

This spec redefines none of the types it uses (FR-161). Owners: content-classes.md (decisions FR-025; Tables F, SQ, FA, OP): `ClassDefinition`, `SubclassDefinition`, `ProgressionRow`, `FeatureEntry`, `FeatureFact` (`AlwaysPreparedSpells`), `StartingEquipment`, `EquipmentLine`, `AbilityRequirement`, `FeatureOption`, `FeatureOptionID`, `OptionPrerequisite` (options are part of the Eldritch Invocations `FeatureEntry`, not a `ContentKind`). content (decisions FR-005, FR-018): `DamageType`, `ConditionKind`, `Skill`, `CreatureSize`, `CreatureType`, the typed ids and `MissingContent(kind, id)`; `LightLevel` and `SpecialSense` (content-schema-and-decoder.md FR-133, FR-136), `SenseEntry` (content-monsters.md FR-027); `Spell`, `Spell.Classes`, `SpellLevel`, `SpellRange` (content-spells.md FR-016, FR-022, FR-036). the kernel packages (decisions FR-001): `Modifier`, `RollMode`, `Ability`, `AbilityModifier`, `dice.Expression`, `DomainEvent`; `Advantage` (modifiers-and-advantage.md FR-034). `d20` (d20-tests.md): `d20.Kind`, `RollModifiers`, `DifficultyClass`, `AttackRollResult`. `character`: `FeatureStates`, `FeatureState`, `FeatureStateKey[T]`, `ClassCreationChoices`, `NoClassCreationChoices`, `CharacterID`, `CharacterEvent` (character-aggregate.md FR-085, FR-093, FR-094); `HitDicePool`, `ClassLevels` (derived-stats.md); `RestKind`, `RestApplied`, `SpentHitDice` (rests-and-hit-dice.md FR-069); `Subclass` with a nil error, an error (level-advancement-and-multiclassing.md); `SpellcastingState`, `PactMagicSlots`, `SpellSlotPool`, `SpellSaveDc`, `CastMode` (spellcasting.md); the host applies `WarlockSlotChange` with `ExpendSpellSlot` (FR-109) and `RegainSpellSlot` (FR-110).

### 5.2 Character domain (`character/`)

Every item is pure and synchronous, holds no collaborator and is constructible from plain values.

- **State** as `FeatureState` implementations under `WarlockFeatureKeys` (FR-158): `WarlockSelections` (chosen `InvocationPick`s, Book contents, familiar form, arcanum spells, the `SubclassID` mirror, the Fiendish Resilience `DamageType`; the `New` constructor validates against the level, FR-005, FR-006, FR-043 to FR-050, FR-098, FR-099, FR-118; cantrips and prepared spells are spellcasting.md's); `WarlockResources` (Magical Cunning, Contact Patron, arcanum uses, Gift of the Depths cast, Gift of the Protectors names and spent state, Dark One's Own Luck uses, Hurl Through Hell, whether a Fiendish Resilience choice is allowed now; no slot, the pool is `PactMagicSlots`); `PactWeaponBond`, `BookOfShadows` (with its optional Gift of the Protectors page), `FamiliarPact` (read by `combat`), `SensorLink`.
- **`WarlockCreationChoices`** (FR-159): skills and Starting Equipment option as `EquipmentLine`s and gold (FR-009). **`InvocationEligibility`** evaluates an `OptionPrerequisite` against the Warlock level, known `FeatureOptionID`s and known cantrips with their `CantripFacts` (FR-045 to FR-048).
- **`CantripFacts`** (FR-156): `spellID`, `effect` (`CantripEffect`: `noDamage`, `damage`, `damageViaAttackRoll`, so an attack roll without damage is unrepresentable), `range` (`SpellRange`). `CantripKind` (`dealsDamage` is met by `damage` and `damageViaAttackRoll`; `dealsDamageViaAttackRoll` only by the latter).
- **Domain services** (stateless): `WarlockAlwaysPrepared` (FR-034, FR-126), `MagicalCunningRestore`, `EldritchSpearRange`, `EldritchSmiteDice`, `LifedrinkerHealing`, `PactWeaponAttackCount`, `GiftOfTheProtectorsCapacity`, `DarkOnesBlessing`, `DarkOnesOwnLuckUses`, `HurlThroughHell` effect data. **Feature functions**: the operations of 6.2.

Invariants and enforcing constructors: counts and membership of invocations and arcanum (`WarlockSelections` constructor and the `New` constructor); uses never above the maximum nor below zero (`WarlockResources`); Hit Point Die roll positive and Charisma modifier within ability-scores.md bounds. Untrusted host input returns a closed rejection; constructors returns an error satisfying `errors.Is(err, srderr.ErrInvalidInput)`; missing content is a `<UseCase>ContentMissing` variant; port failure is `*srderr.PortError` (`doc/rules/architecture.md` §6).

## 6. Interface

Two kinds of operation (decisions FR-023, FR-160): **domain functions** (6.2) in `character/`, pure and synchronous, taking the Warlock state values they read (from `FeatureStates`, FR-158) and plain inputs and returning a closed result with the new state values and events (writing back into the `Character` is the aggregate's job); and **use cases** (6.1) in `character/`, only where a feature needs content or dice through a port: load content or roll, call the matching domain function with the value, map the outcome, no rules logic; `context.Context`-taking, error-returning only if it calls `content.Source`.

Naming and rejection shape (decisions FR-017, FR-021; FR-147, FR-162): every operation has its own closed `<Operation>Result` with variants `<Operation>Applied(...)`, `<Operation>Rejected(reason, detail)` and, for content-loading use cases, `<Operation>ContentMissing(missing)` (`MissingContent(kind, id)` held as a field); generic names are never exported. `reason` is the `<Operation>Reason` enum below; `detail` is a closed `<Operation>Detail` with one variant per reason that carries data and `<Operation>DetailNone` for the rest, so it is never nil. Requirements name reasons in PascalCase (`SkillCountWrong`); the enum constant is PascalCase and its `String()` and telemetry value snake_case. A use case's result differs from its function's only by the content-missing variant. `warlockLevel` is 0 for a character with no Warlock levels (FR-143). Every operation that returns events takes `characterID` (FR-164).

## 7. Observability

Only the eight use cases of 6.1 emit; the domain functions are silent by design (they return what happened as results and events, decisions FR-023). Warlock features use the shared signals owned by character-aggregate.md (FR-118, FR-119; decisions FR-042, FR-063): counter `srd5e.character.feature_used`, histogram `srd5e.character.feature_used.duration` and counter `srd5e.character.feature_used.errors`, with `srd5e.class` = `warlock` and `srd5e.feature` = the use case value below. This spec declares only the spans, no metric, no log and no per-feature name. Attribute values follow decisions FR-041; the exact recording rules are FR-148.

Evaluation (`doc/rules/observability.md` §2): *What can go wrong?* Warlock content missing or malformed; `content.Source` or `dice.Roller` failing; a request illegal in the state (spent, below level, outside a rest, prerequisite unmet); invalid host input; programmer errors (`srderr.ErrInvalidInput`). *How would an operator know?* Span error status; a returned port error increments `feature_used.errors` with `error.type`; a content-missing result is `failure` with rejection `content_missing`, no `error.type`, and sets span error status; a rejection is `failure` with the reason and no error status. *What to diagnose with?* Low-cardinality `srd5e.class`, `srd5e.feature`, outcome and rejection; the result carries the roll, counts and resulting state. *What to count or time?* Uses and latency per feature on the shared signals. *Is existing instrumentation still correct?* No Warlock signals existed; the old `srd5e.character.warlock.*` metrics, the spec-local `feature_duration` histogram and the debug log `feature_resolved` are removed (decisions FR-042, FR-063; the log only restated the span and counter). *What to monitor?* Any `feature_used.errors` increment for `warlock`; a rise in `content_missing`; a sudden rise in one rejection for one feature (for example `not_after_rest` on `conjure_book_of_shadows`); a latency regression per feature. *Is anything noisy?* No; nothing is logged in domain code or use cases.

| Signal | Type | Attributes | Purpose | Suggested monitoring |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `srd5e.character.<feature>` | span, one per use case (the naming rule of other class specs, class-sorcerer.md); `<feature>` is `choose_warlock_invocations`, `choose_mystic_arcanum`, `choose_warlock_familiar_form`, `conjure_book_of_shadows`, `bond_pact_weapon`, `cast_invocation_spell`, `use_lifedrinker` or `use_dark_ones_own_luck`; content-loading ones have the child span `srd5e.character.load_content` (character-aggregate.md) | `srd5e.class`, `srd5e.feature`, `telemetry.AttrOutcome`, `srd5e.character.rejection` (failures from a rejection or content-missing only) | Trace one use case, including the `dice.Roller` roll for Lifedrinker and Dark One's Own Luck | Error status; rejection mix by reason (`light_level`, `not_after_rest`); content missing for a monster id |

Failure recording: the use case counts and records a failure once, with no log; a rejected request is an outcome, not an error (FR-148). Names and keys are constants in `character/warlock_telemetry.go`. Entries feed `doc/observability/signals.md`.

## 9. Open questions and decisions

Settled, one line each: telemetry names and the feature histogram (decisions FR-041, FR-042, FR-063; character-aggregate.md FR-118); option shape and ids (content-classes.md FR-061, FR-062); slot-free casting (casting-and-concentration.md FR-121); Q-8 an always-prepared spell is not replaceable (spellcasting.md FR-046); `WarlockEvent` as its own `CharacterEvent` family (character-aggregate.md FR-085); Q-9 the timing of Dark One's Own Luck is the host's responsibility (FR-127); Lifedrinker's spent die (rests-and-hit-dice.md FR-069; this spec's Q-22, FR-078); Q-2 Mystic Arcanum spells are held in their own list, not prepared (FR-119).

Settled by this revision: **Q-3** an arcanum spell is never also a prepared spell, because arcanum levels 6 to 9 exceed the largest Slot Level of 5, the cap on prepared spells (SRD 5.2.1, "Warlock Features", p. 71; FR-032). **Q-4** `CantripFacts` stays a pure-domain input of this spec with table 4.6.8 as its fixture; no schema request is open (package design, content-classes.md D3). **Q-5** Agonizing Blast adds a negative Charisma modifier as written (p. 72 has no floor; FR-055). **Q-6** Magical Cunning with no expended slot still uses it up ("Once you use this feature", p. 72; FR-108). **Q-7** a known cantrip counts as already prepared for the Book of Shadows (interpretation; FR-099). **Q-14** a normal familiar form is a Beast of Challenge Rating 0 checked on its monster entry ("Find Familiar", p. 130; FR-090). **Q-15** Book of Shadows spells count against neither the Prepared Spells number nor cantrips known (interpretation; FR-100). **Q-16** Devouring Blade gives 3 pact weapon attacks over a baseline below 3 (interpretation: it is the "feature that says you can", p. 25; FR-155). **Q-17** `Sight`, `Unlimited` and `Special` ranges do not qualify as 10+ feet (FR-157). **Q-18** True Strike is `damageViaAttackRoll` with range Self, so eligible for Agonizing Blast and Repelling Blast and not for Eldritch Spear (interpretation: it makes a weapon attack, p. 171; CC-07). **Q-21** only the eight use cases are counted; the domain functions stay silent by design (decisions FR-023, FR-160). **Q-22** `WarlockSlotChange` stays as the interface: the host applies it with `ExpendSpellSlot` and `RegainSpellSlot` (FR-163); no spellcasting.md change is needed.
