## 1. Purpose and scope

Creatures that a spell or feature brings or calls, and how they behave in combat: ally status, where they sit in the turn order, how they are commanded, when they disappear, and the numbers of the four stat blocks that depend on the slot level (Animated Object, Otherworldly Steed, Giant Insect, Draconic Spirit). Also Animate Dead and Create Undead (the 24-hour control window and reasserting it), Simulacrum, Find Familiar (one at a time, dismiss and recall, telepathic connection, delivering a touch), and Find Steed. The familiar is one shared entity: Find Familiar cast as a spell, Pact of the Chain and Wild Companion differ only by `FamiliarOrigin` (FR-043, FR-053, FR-054).

State in, new state and events out (`architecture.md` §4). Pure rules live in stateless package functions and methods of `SummonedRoster` over an immutable `SummonedRoster` the host stores beside the `Encounter`, as it does a grapple record. Two thin use cases wrap them (FR-070 to FR-072). The engine models no map: distances, unoccupied spaces, night and size facts arrive in commands.

**Ownership (`shared-types-and-ownership.md` FR-048).** Owned here: `SummonedRoster`, `SummonedLink`, `SummonKind`, `SummonedStatBlock` and its level expressions, command and control rules, departure rules, `FamiliarOrigin`. Referenced, never redefined: `CombatantID`, `combat.Event` (`combat-foundations.md`); `HitPointStatus`, `DamageResolution`, `DamageTaken` (`hit-points-and-damage.md`); `BonusActionGrant`, `FeatureReaction`, `ActionAvailability`, `InitiativeCount`, `CombatantProfile` (`initiative-and-actions.md`); `MountFacts`, `MountTraining` (`mounted-and-underwater-combat.md`); `RosterPosition` (`RosterAfterCombatant`, `RosterAtInitiative`), `TiePlacement`, `JoinReason`, `RemovalCause`, `CombatantJoined`, `CombatantRemoved`, `AddCombatantToEncounter`, `RemoveCombatantFromEncounter`, `RosterCombatantIsActiveRejection` (`combatant-roster-changes.md`); `TelepathyCapability` (`telepathy.md`); `ConcentrationEnded` (`casting-and-concentration.md` FR-141); `FamiliarForm` (`class-warlock.md` FR-090); `MonsterID`, `Monster`, `CreatureSize`, `CreatureType`, `DamageType`, `SpellID` (`content`). Telepathy rules stay in `telepathy.md`; this spec only supplies the capability and range (FR-045, FR-058). Class riders (Investment of the Chain Master, Forgo an attack, Wild Companion's slot payment) stay in `class-warlock.md` FR-089 to FR-096 and `class-druid.md` FR-086 to FR-091. No use case calls another (`shared-types-and-ownership.md` FR-044).

**Out of scope:** casting itself (slots, components, casting time, Concentration: `casting-and-concentration.md`); the spell text, which stays `EmbeddedStatBlock` text in `content-spells.md` FR-069 (this spec holds the numbers a combat needs, FR-037); Finger of Death's rising Zombie and Polymorph's "Object into Creature" (text, `spell-effect-data.md` E19); magic-item summons (Brazier of Commanding Fire Elementals and similar, `content-magic-items.md`); Vampire Familiar and monster traits that call a creature (`content-monsters.md`); object hit points and what the overflow does to an object (`environment-hazards-and-objects.md`; this spec returns the overflow amount, FR-021); adding, removing and placing a combatant in the `TurnTracker` and `Encounter` (owned by `combatant-roster-changes.md`; this spec only returns the `RosterPosition` and `RemovalCause` the host passes to it); Monster limited use of Fell Glare, Fey Step and Healing Touch (`monster-limited-use.md`); Wild Shape; the GM's choice of how a lapsed undead behaves (no SRD rule, FR-040). Nothing outside SRD 5.2.1 was needed; the SRD gives no Initiative rule for Animate Dead or Create Undead creatures (FR-003, Q-3).

## 5. Model

Notation: `Name(a, b)` lists the fields of a struct type `Name`; a "closed" type is an interface with an unexported marker method, a doc comment naming every variant, and one struct per variant, switched exhaustively (`errors-and-results.md` FR-018). Constructors are `NewX` returning `(T, error)` with errors satisfying `errors.Is(err, srderr.ErrInvalidInput)`; `MustX` panics with that error. Slices and maps are copied on construction and on read.

All in package `combat`, in files named `summoned*.go` (the use cases in `summon_creature_usecase.go` and `apply_summoned_change_usecase.go`); the domain files are pure (no ports, `context.Context` or clock), immutable (unexported fields, value receivers, accessors without a Get prefix) and comparable where their fields allow.

- **Aggregate-like state:** `SummonedRoster` (defensive copies of slices of `SummonedLink`; at most one `SummonFamiliar`, one `SummonOtherworldlySteed` and one `SummonSimulacrum` per summoner, enforced by the transitions, FR-044, FR-056, FR-063). `SummonedLink`: `creature`, `summoner`, `kind`, `spell` (`SpellID`), `CastLevel`, `presence` (`present`, `InPocketDimension`), `controlWindow?` (undead only), `standingOrder?`, `retainedPosition?` (familiar, a `RosterAtInitiative`), `PlacementRule` (`SharesSummonerCount`, `OwnCount`), `familiar?` (`FamiliarState`: form, type, `FamiliarOrigin`, sight link end), `DesignatedFriends` (simulacrum).
- **Value objects:** `SummonedStatBlock` (`MonsterID`, `CreatureSize`, `CreatureType`, armor class, `HitPointMaximum`, speeds, affinities, condition immunities, attacks, `ProficiencyBonus`, `ForbiddenSpells`, `CanGainLevels`, `CanTakeRests`, `XP`), `SummonedAttack`, `SummonerSpellFacts` (attack bonus, save DC, spellcasting modifier, `ProficiencyBonus`), `SummonedRosterJoin`, `SummonedRosterLeave` (FR-077; `RosterPosition`, `JoinReason`, `RemovalCause` are referenced, not defined), `SharedResistanceGrant`, `CommandChannel`, `SummonedOrder`, `SummonedTurnPlan`, `ControlWindow`, `FamiliarOrigin` (`FamiliarOriginFindFamiliarSpell`, `FamiliarOriginPactOfTheChain`, `FamiliarOriginWildCompanion`), `SteedType`, `SteedMode`, `SteedChoice`, `OverflowDamage`, `SummonEndCause` (`DroppedToZeroHitPoints`, `SpellEnded`, `SummonerDied`, `LongRestFinished`, `DismissedForever`, `DismissedTemporarily`, `replaced`, `ControlLapsed`), `FamiliarAttackGrant`.
- **Pure functions** (stateless; no service types): the four stat block constructors of FR-029 to FR-034 (Table S) and the transitions as methods of `SummonedRoster` (`Summon`, `Reassert`, `IssueCommand`, `ReportZeroHitPoints`, `ReportSpellEnded`, `ReportSummonerDied`, `ReportLongRestFinished`, `ElapseControl`, `Dismiss`, `Recall`, `ShareSenses`, `DeliverTouch`, `Repair`, `LifeBond`) plus `BonusActionGrantFor`, each returning a `SummonedTransition` (a closed variant set: an interface with an unexported marker method and the variants `SummonedApplied{Roster, Events, RosterActions}`, `SummonedRejected{Rejection}`, `SummonedUnchanged`).
- **Invariants and where enforced:** a cast level in 1 to 9, non-negative durations, non-empty unique targets: the command constructors (`New...Command` returning `(T, error)` that is `errors.Is` `srderr.ErrInvalidInput`, FR-067); a link names two distinct ids: `SummonedLink`; one familiar, steed and simulacrum per summoner: the transitions (FR-044, FR-056, FR-063); a lapsed window never revives: `ControlWindow` (FR-040, FR-041).
- **Failures:** expected outcomes are results (rejections, `SummonCreatureContentMissing`, `SummonedUnchanged`); invalid untrusted input is a returned error that is `errors.Is` `srderr.ErrInvalidInput`; the only collaborator failure is the `content.Source` port, returned as a `*srderr.PortError` for port `content` (FR-071). No panic outside `Must...` helpers.

## 6. Interface

| Use case | Ports | Command (fields; invariants) | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| `SummonCreature` (`Do(ctx, cmd)`; I/O through `content.Source`) | `content.Source`, `telemetry.Telemetry` | `SummonCreatureCommand{Roster, Summoner, Side, Request SummonRequest, Facts SummonerSpellFacts, InCombat, IsNight, space reported, incoming ids}`; requests (variants of the closed `SummonRequest`): animate objects, giant insect, summon dragon, find steed, animate dead, create undead, find familiar, simulacrum, `ReassertControl`) | `(SummonCreatureResult, error)`: `SummonCreatureApplied`, `SummonCreatureRejected`, `SummonCreatureContentMissing`; the error is a `*srderr.PortError` or a cancellation |
| `ApplySummonedChange` (`Do(ctx, cmd)`; no I/O, `ctx` only for the span) | `telemetry.Telemetry` | `ApplySummonedChangeCommand{Roster, Change SummonedChange}` | `(ApplySummonedChangeResult, error)`: `ApplySummonedChangeApplied`, `ApplySummonedChangeRejected`, `ApplySummonedChangeUnchanged` |

- **Why a `content.Source` here:** only the undead and familiar forms need a stat block that is content; the four level expressions are rules maths (FR-037). **Port failure contract:** an absent id is `SummonCreatureContentMissing` (a result); a source failure is returned as a `*srderr.PortError`. `content.Source.Get(ctx, ...)` takes `ctx`, so `SummonCreature` takes `ctx` first and returns synchronously; the roster transitions stay pure and context-free.
- **Exports:** package `combat` exports the two use cases, commands, results, `SummonedRoster`, `SummonEvent` and its events, the value objects above and the `...Rejection` types with the closed `SummonRejection`; the stat block constructors and the `SummonedRoster` methods are exported as pure functions (FR-075). No new test double: `contenttest.FakeSource` and the recording `telemetrytest` doubles already exist.

## 7. Observability

1. **What can go wrong:** a missing stat block (content), a failing `content.Source`, a refused summon (level, night, eligibility), a command to a lapsed or foreign creature, a double departure. 2. **How an operator knows:** the shared `srd5e.combat.operations` counter with `srd5e.outcome` and `srd5e.combat.result`; `ContentMissing` is class `invalid` (span error), a port failure is counted in `srd5e.combat.errors` with `error.type`. 3. **To diagnose:** the result carries the rejection and the events carry kind and cause. 4. **Counted:** summons by kind and endings by cause, below. 5. **Existing instrumentation:** `srd5e.combat.events` counts each `SummonEvent` kind (FR-069); nothing is misleading. 6. **Monitor:** a rising `summon_endings` with cause `control_lapsed` (hosts forgetting to reassert) and any `content_missing` result for `SummonCreature`. 7. **Noise:** none; no log at all (rule outcomes are results).

| Signal | Type | Attributes | Purpose | Suggested monitoring |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `srd5e.combat.summons` | counter `{creature}` | `srd5e.combat.summon_kind` (`animated_object`, `giant_insect`, `draconic_spirit`, `otherworldly_steed`, `animated_undead`, `created_undead`, `familiar`, `simulacrum`) | How many creatures each spell brings; incremented once per creature on `SummonCreatureApplied` | Trend per kind; a drop to 0 after a release |
| `srd5e.combat.summon_endings` | counter `{creature}` | `srd5e.combat.summon_kind`, `srd5e.combat.summon_end_cause` (the snake_case of `SummonEndCause`) | Why summoned creatures leave; once per `SummonedCreatureDeparted` or `SummonControlLapsed` | Alert on a high share of `control_lapsed` |
| `srd5e.combat.summon_creature` / `.apply_summoned_change` | span | the shared four (`combat-foundations.md` FR-029 to FR-035) | One span per use case | Latency of `summon_creature` (content lookup) |

Result values (`srd5e.combat.result`): `summon_creature`: `summoned`, `form_changed`, `replaced`, `control_reasserted`, `rejected_<reason>` (reason names of Table R, snake_case), `content_missing`. `apply_summoned_change`: `commanded`, `departed`, `lapsed`, `dismissed`, `recalled`, `repaired`, `touch_delivered`, `senses_shared`, `life_bond`, `unchanged`, `rejected_<reason>`. Both new attribute keys and values are added to `combat-foundations.md` FR-041 (amendment A6). The domain is silent.

## 9. Open questions and decisions

No open question needs escalation; each interpretation below rests on SRD text and ordinary table ruling (web research found no official erratum that conflicts).

**Settled, one line each:** Q-1 Simulacrum "acts on your turn": modelled directly after the summoner, one active combatant per turn (interpretation). Q-2 Summoned creatures are `MonsterCombatant`; `CombatantKind` is identity only (`combat-foundations.md` FR-012), so no new kind. Q-3 Animate Dead, Create Undead and the familiar have no shared count in the text, so each has its own Initiative (standard rule, p. 13). Q-4 Orders are consumed per turn except undead, whose text says the order is followed "until its task is complete" (interpretation). Q-5 Create Undead rows are exclusive alternatives; Ghasts and Wights share one count (interpretation). Q-6 A form change gives the new form's maximum Hit Points because the text says it adopts the form's statistics and is silent on Hit Points (interpretation, consistent with the stat block governing). Q-7 The spell's telepathic connection is the glossary Telepathy, so its Incapacitated and range breaks apply (interpretation). Q-8 A steed that disappeared returns at full Hit Points since the stat block is rebuilt at the cast level (interpretation). Q-9 A lapsed undead stays an uncontrolled creature; its conduct is the GM's (no SRD rule). Q-10 The summoning spells' Concentration, slots and casting time stay in `casting-and-concentration.md`. Q-11 Summoned creature XP follows p. 256, so the four expression creatures award 0. Q-12 Find Steed: the SRD gives "immediately after yours" only while the summoner is Incapacitated, so the other states use the same place by package consistency with `combatant-roster-changes.md` FR-013; the ridden turn is the controlled-mount rule (interpretation). Q-13 Animate Objects ends control "until the spell ends" and states reversion only at 0 Hit Points, so objects are modelled as reverting at spell end (interpretation; the GM may rule otherwise). Q-14 A simulacrum uses `DiesInstantly` because it lasts "until it drops to 0 Hit Points" (package design; affinities stay the original's). Q-15 A recast familiar takes the latest cast's origin, since Wild Companion's Fey and Long Rest rider belong to a familiar cast "in this way" (interpretation). Q-16 Half the original's Hit Points rounds down per p. 187 with a minimum of 1 as package design. Q-17 The 100-foot distance gates sight-sharing as one Telepathic Connection feature (interpretation; web research found community consensus, no official erratum). Q-18 The Shared Resistances choice and the Breath Weapon type are two choices (literal reading of p. 166). Q-19 A departing active combatant is removed after its turn because the roster spec refuses to remove it (package design).

**Amendments requested (A3 to A6)** are returned to the orchestrator and not applied here (A1 and A2, tracker and encounter roster operations, are delivered by `combatant-roster-changes.md` and withdrawn): A3 rank 31 in `shared-types-and-ownership.md` 4.11 and a row in `documentation_index.md`; A4 prose pointers in `class-warlock.md` FR-089/FR-090 and `class-druid.md` FR-090; A5 a pointer from `spell-effect-data.md` E19 and `content-spells.md` FR-069 to FR-037; A6 `combat-foundations.md` FR-041 attribute additions.
