## 1. Purpose and scope

Models how a character casts a spell and how Concentration works: the casting rules the SRD states in "Spells" (slot use, casting time, components, Rituals, casting in armor, longer casting times, casts granted by a feature without a slot), Concentration (beginning, ending, breaking, and the save DC after damage because the SRD states it with Concentration), and the use cases `CastSpell`, `CompleteCasting`, `BreakConcentration` and `BeginConcentration`. It was split from `spellcasting.md` (shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-062); every moved id keeps its number, so a bare `FR-nnn` that is defined in section 4 below is this spec's and any other bare `FR-nnn` is `spellcasting.md`'s (its section 4 "Withdrawn ids" lists the moved ones). New ids of the pair start at FR-141 (this spec uses FR-141 to FR-148; `spellcasting.md` ends at FR-140).

This spec owns the Concentration and casting *rules* (shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-011); `spellcasting.md` owns `SpellcastingState` and the shapes it holds. The save-DC rule takes a plain `int`, never a `combat` event (FR-079). A `combat` use case reads the character's Concentration state, passes the damage in, rolls the save and composes the published pure break transition (FR-128); it calls no use case of this context (architecture.md §4; shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-044). The roll is a D20 Test: a use case composes the pure `d20.Resolver` (d20-tests.md FR-069), a host calls `ResolveD20Test` (resolve-d20-test-use-case.md). State in, new state and events out (`doc/rules/architecture.md` §4).

Reused, not redefined (architecture.md §2; shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-001 to FR-012): `ClassLevels`, `CharacterLevel` (derived-stats.md FR-058, FR-059); `DifficultyClass` (d20-tests.md FR-021); `SpellID`, `FeatureID`, `ClassID` (content-schema-and-decoder.md FR-128); `CharacterEvent`, `CharacterID` (character-aggregate.md FR-085); from content-spells.md `Spell`, `SpellLevel`, `CastingTime`, `SpellCastingTimes`, `CastingTimeMode`, `SpellComponents`, `MaterialComponent`, `SpellDuration`; from spellcasting.md the types of the dependency note.

**Out of scope:**

- The spells themselves (lists, descriptions, effects, SRD 5.2.1, p. 106): the published `Spell` (content-spells.md). Preparation, slots, stats and level changes: `spellcasting.md`.
- Whether a feature grants a free cast, and how often (Smite, Mystic Arcanum, Contact Patron, Pact of the Chain, Divine Intervention, Dragon Companion, Spell Mastery, Signature Spells): entitlement and counting stay with the feature owner; the cast is in scope through `CastMode.FeatureGranted` (FR-121 to FR-124), and the standalone slot operations other features need are `spellcasting.md` FR-109, FR-110.
- Whether a caster can cast because of a form or state (Wild Shape, p. 43; Rage, p. 29): the caller reports `CanCastSpells` and any Concentration loss (FR-113, FR-119). Bonus Action and Reaction timing beyond the trigger fact (FR-069) is combat territory.
- Which scroll or magic item may be read, its terms and what happens to it afterwards ("Casting without Slots", p. 104): `spell-scroll-use.md` and `magic-item-rules.md`; the cast itself is `CastMode.SpellScroll` here (FR-143). Spellbook management (p. 78); any `combat` type (damage arrives as a plain `int`, FR-079, the ending condition as a reason, FR-076); monster Spellcasting traits (`monster-spellcasting.md`; it builds a `CastReport` through FR-147); "Identifying an Ongoing Spell" (p. 106); Armor Class and Shield proficiency (the caller supplies `UntrainedArmorWorn`, true only for untrained Light, Medium or Heavy armor; an untrained Shield does not block casting, p. 177, FR-063).
- Optional rules: the SRD names none for spellcasting, so no `RuleOptions`.

## 5. Model

Everything here is domain code in package `character`, per `doc/rules/architecture.md` §3: immutable (unexported fields, value receivers), pure, synchronous, free of dice and time, no `context.Context`. Upstream types are listed in section 1 and not redefined.

**Value objects**

- Enums: `ComponentKind` (`verbal`, `somatic`, `material`); `CastMode` (`normal`, `ritual`, `FeatureGranted`, `SpellScroll`, `duplicated`); `ConcentrationBreakReason` (`incapacitated`, `dead`, `ended`, `FailedSave`, `AnotherEffect`; FR-119); `ConcentrationEndReason` (those and `CastingCompleted`, produced only by `CompleteCasting`).
- `ConcentrationSaveDC` wraps a `DifficultyClass`; `ConcentrationSaveDCForDamage(int)` is the published save-DC rule (FR-078, FR-079). `ConcentrationBreakTransition{State, Events, CastingFailed}` with an optional `CastingFailed` (FR-128); `CounterCastTransition{State, Events}` (FR-145); `SpellCastTerms{SaveDC, AttackBonus, CantripScalingLevel}` with an optional `CantripScalingLevel` (FR-143).
- `CastReport` (FR-082, FR-147: casting class, slot, DC and attack bonus may be absent; `CountsAsSlotSpell`); `CastingFailed` (spell id and the `ConcentrationEndReason`, never `CastingCompleted`; also a `CastingEvent`); `CastingCountered(spell)` (FR-145).
- `CastingContext`: `SlotSpentThisTurn`, `UntrainedArmorWorn`, `CanCastSpells`, `CanSpeak`, `CanMakeGestures`, `HasFreeHand`, `HasMaterials`, `HasComponentPouch`, `HeldFocus` (optional `SpellcastingFocusKind`), `FeatureFocusKinds` (feature-granted kinds only, default empty; FR-129), `CanReadSpellbook` (FR-130), `TriggerMet`, `UnavailableSpells` (`set of SpellID`, default empty; FR-146). Each rule reads exactly the facts its FR names (FR-063 to FR-069, FR-095, FR-113, FR-130); a held focus does not read `HasFreeHand` (FR-095).
- From `spellcasting.md`, used and not redefined: `Concentration`, `ConcentrationSubject`, `ConcentrationKind` (FR-118), `CastSource` (derived by `CastSpell`, never input: FR-106, FR-121; spellcasting.md must add the variants `scroll` and `monster`, FR-143, FR-147, and a nullable class on `CastingInProgress`), `SpellSlotChoice`, `CastingInProgress` (FR-070 to FR-072, FR-131), `SpellcastingState`. The spell input is the content context's `Spell`; this spec reads `level`, `CastingTimes`, `ritual`, `components` (`ConsumesMaterial`, `CostsGp`), `classes` and `RequiresConcentration`.

**Domain services** (stateless, plain values only, so unit tested with no port, double or `telemetry.Telemetry`; `doc/rules/testing.md` §3):

- `SpellCasting`: a cast and its completion (FR-055 to FR-083, FR-101 to FR-108, FR-113 to FR-115, FR-121 to FR-125, FR-130, FR-138 to FR-140, FR-143, FR-144, FR-146), returning internal `CastOutcome` (`CastPerformed(report, state, events)` | `CastBegun(state, events)` | `CastRefused(rejection)`). It uses the internal `ConcentrationTransitions` (begin and completion; FR-075, FR-101, FR-120, FR-138), where the `CastingFailed` of a `BeginConcentration` call is produced.
- `ConcentrationRules` (published, stateless, zero-value usable; FR-128, FR-145): `BreakConcentration(SpellcastingState, ConcentrationBreakReason)` returning the exported `ConcentrationBreakTransition` (FR-072, FR-076, FR-077, FR-099, FR-119) and `CounterCast(SpellcastingState, Spell)` returning `CounterCastTransition` (FR-145). A `combat` use case composes it (combat-action-resolution.md FR-003, FR-078); the `BreakConcentration` use case returns its result unchanged. The save-DC rule is on the value object `ConcentrationSaveDC`, not this service, so downstream `combat` domain code can use it (FR-079).

`CastOutcome` and `ConcentrationTransitions` are not exported. Each use case maps its outcome to its own `<UseCase>Result` (shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-017).

**Rejections.** Reasons are enum values in rejection value objects, so kinds in two lists never collide in the package. `CastRejection` holds an ordered list of `CastRejectionKind`; fixed reporting order (FR-080): `CannotCastSpells`, `SpellUnavailable` (FR-146), `UntrainedArmor`, `CastingAlreadyInProgress`, `NotKnown` / `NotPrepared` / `NotInSpellbook`, `SpellbookNotReadable` (FR-130), `NotARitual`, `TriggerNotMet`, `MissingComponent(verbal)`, `MissingComponent(somatic)`, `MissingComponent(material)`, `SlotAlreadySpentThisTurn`, `SlotLevelTooLow`, `NoSlotAvailable`. `CastingNotInProgress` (FR-105) is the only reason of a `CompleteCasting` with nothing to complete and is never listed with another. A feature-granted cast (FR-122, FR-140) can produce only `CannotCastSpells`, `SpellUnavailable`, `UntrainedArmor`, `CastingAlreadyInProgress`, `TriggerNotMet`, `MissingComponent` kinds and, with `CountsAsSlotSpell`, `SlotAlreadySpentThisTurn` (FR-144), in that relative order; a scroll cast (FR-143) only the first five and `MissingComponent(verbal)` and `(somatic)`. The PascalCase kinds of section 4 are the camelCase enum values.

**Naming of results.** Every result is closed and named `<UseCase>Result`.

| Use case | Result and variants (exported) | Shorthand in section 4 and the tables |
|---|---|---|
| `CastSpell` | `CastSpellCast(report, state, events)`, `CastSpellStarted(state, events)`, `CastSpellRejected(rejection)` | `SpellCast`, `CastingStarted`, `CastRejected(...)` |
| `CompleteCasting` | `CompleteCastingCompleted(report, state, events)`, `CompleteCastingRejected(rejection)` | `SpellCast`, `CastRejected(...)` |
| `BreakConcentration` | `BreakConcentrationEnded(state, events)`, `...CastingFailed(state, events, castingFailed)`, `...Unchanged(state)`; built from the transition of FR-128 | `CastingFailed` |
| `BeginConcentration` | `BeginConcentrationBegan(state, events)`, `...CastingFailed(state, events, castingFailed)` | `CastingFailed` |

**Events** (past tense, FR-083): members of the closed variant family `CastingEvent` (FR-141): `SpellCast(spell, class?, source, castAtLevel, scalingLevel)`, `CastingCountered(spell)` (FR-145), `ConcentrationBegan(subject, kind)`, `ConcentrationEnded(subject, reason)` (`ConcentrationEndReason`) and `CastingFailed(spell, reason)`. `SpellSlotExpended` is `spellcasting.md`'s and appears in the same `events` list, typed `[]CharacterEvent`. `CastingFailed` is in the `events` of a use case that ends a casting, directly after the `ConcentrationEnded` that ended it (FR-083, FR-128); the `CastingFailed` field of the transition and of `BreakConcentrationCastingFailed` / `BeginConcentrationCastingFailed` is that same value repeated, not a second event.

**Failures.** Rule outcomes (rejections, `CompleteCasting` with nothing in progress (FR-105), `NoSlotAvailable`, `CastingFailed`) are closed results. Constructor violations and contradictory command input (FR-106, FR-108, FR-115, FR-124, FR-125, FR-134, FR-143 to FR-145) are validation errors returned by the `NewX` constructors (`errors.Is` `srderr.ErrInvalidInput`, typed `*InvalidError` with a `Reason`; errors-and-results.md FR-027), never a panic outside `MustX`. No `srderr.PortError` is returned (no port does I/O).

## 6. Interface

Synchronous use cases (a type per use case with `Run(ctx context.Context, cmd <UseCase>Command) (<UseCase>Result, error)`; `ctx` is first because the use cases emit spans, and the only possible `error` is `ctx.Err()`), each with an exported immutable `<UseCase>Command` value object (architecture.md §4, go.md §4):

| Use case | Command | Result |
|---|---|---|
| `CastSpell` | `SpellcastingState`, class (optional `ClassID`, absent only for `SpellScroll` and a class-less `FeatureGranted` cast, FR-149), optional `SpellcastingStats` (FR-149), `Spell`, `CastMode`, optional `SpellSlotChoice`, optional `CastingTimeMode`, `CastingContext`, optional `SpellCastTerms` (`SpellScroll` only), optional `featureCastLevel`, `CountsAsSlotSpell` (`FeatureGranted` only) | `CastSpellResult` and events (FR-055 to FR-083, FR-101 to FR-108, FR-113 to FR-115, FR-121 to FR-124, FR-130, FR-143, FR-144, FR-146, FR-149) |
| `CompleteCasting` | `SpellcastingState`, `Spell`, `CastingContext`, optional `SpellSlotChoice` (replacement), optional `featureCastLevel`, `CountsAsSlotSpell` (FR-144) | `CompleteCastingResult` (FR-071, FR-103 to FR-105, FR-125, FR-130, FR-138, FR-139, FR-144) |
| `BreakConcentration` | `SpellcastingState`, `ConcentrationBreakReason` | the state and events of `ConcentrationRules.BreakConcentration` (FR-128), with `CastingFailed` when a casting was in progress (FR-072, FR-076, FR-077, FR-099, FR-119) |
| `BeginConcentration` | `SpellcastingState`, `ConcentrationSubject` (`.feature(FeatureID)` form, FR-148) | new state, events, and `CastingFailed` when a casting was in progress (FR-118, FR-120, FR-148) |

`ConcentrationSaveDCForDamage(damageTaken int)` (FR-078, FR-079), `ConcentrationRules.BreakConcentration` (FR-128) and `CounterCast` (FR-145) are pure, published, and record nothing (FR-137); they are not use cases. The `combat` context composes them and calls no use case of this context (architecture.md §4; shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-044; combat-action-resolution.md FR-003, FR-007): it reads the `Concentration` from the `SpellcastingState`, takes the damage taken as a plain `int` (FR-135), skips the call when zero (FR-134), calls `ConcentrationSaveDCForDamage`, rolls the Constitution save with `d20.Resolver` (d20-tests.md FR-069) and on a failed save calls `BreakConcentration` with `FailedSave`, returning state and events in its own result. Only a host that does not use those `combat` use cases calls `BreakConcentration` and the `d20` use case. The only handoffs are the `int`, the `DifficultyClass`, the reason, the state and the transition. magical-effects.md withdraws its Concentration section (4.13) to `spellcasting.md`'s Concentration rules, now this spec, and refers to `ConcentrationSaveDCForDamage`.

**Ports.** None new; the caller resolves each `Spell` and passes it in, so the use cases stay synchronous and depend on content only through its published language. The `telemetry.Telemetry` ports are an optional `WithTelemetry` option on the use-case constructors, defaulting to the zero-value `telemetry.Noop` (shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-036; observability.md §4); use cases import no adapter package.

**Exports** (FR-142), from package `character`: every type that a command or result of this section names, so no consumer needs an unexported type: the four use cases, their `<UseCase>Command` and `<UseCase>Result` types with variants, `CastMode`, `CastingContext`, `ComponentKind`, `CastReport`, the rejection value objects and kinds, `ConcentrationRules`, `ConcentrationBreakTransition`, `CounterCastTransition`, `SpellCastTerms`, `ConcentrationSaveDC`, `ConcentrationBreakReason`, `ConcentrationEndReason`, and `CastingEvent` with its events. Not re-exported: other specs' types (section 1). Telemetry name constants live in one `signals.go` file (observability.md §5); `telemetrytest` and `dicetest` gain no double (no port).

## 7. Observability

The domain code is silent (FR-137); the use cases (`*_usecase.go`) emit the signals below (`doc/rules/observability.md` §2). FR-085 to FR-088 (span, outcome counter, duration, no-op equality, validation-error handling, logging limit) hold for these use cases.

- **1-2. What can go wrong:** rule rejections (normal outcomes); invalid untrusted input (a returned validation error, FR-087). No port, so no I/O failure, no `srderr.PortError`, no `error.type` (errors-and-results.md FR-043).
- **6. Operator view:** rejections and successes are values of `srd5e.outcome` on one counter beside the finer `srd5e.character.result`. No spell id, character id or free text is an attribute. Monitor the share of `failure` by `srd5e.character.rejection`, error-status spans and duration regressions.
- **3, 7. Diagnostics and logs:** no `INFO` or above (FR-088); one `FINE` summary for `CastSpell` only; spans and `rejection` values locate a failure. **4. Counted or timed:** casts, per-operation counts and durations. **5. Existing instrumentation:** `operations` and `duration` stay correct; this spec adds operation values only.

Shared signals this spec adds values to: `srd5e.character.operations` (the operation values `break_concentration` and `begin_concentration`) and `srd5e.character.duration` (the four use-case names here), both owned by character-aggregate.md section 7; it uses `srd5e.outcome` (shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-041) and `srd5e.class` (FR-042) and emits no `srd5e.character.feature_used`. No use case calls another, so no span nests and nothing is recorded twice (shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-044). Every use case has a span `srd5e.character.<operation>` with `srd5e.outcome`, `srd5e.character.result`, `srd5e.character.operation` and the attributes below. `result` values: `rejected` is `failure`; `cast`, `started`, `ended`, `began`, `none`, `casting_failed` are `success` (`casting_failed` did what was asked, the dependent casting failing as the SRD says; `none` is a no-op). A rule rejection sets no error status (FR-085). `CastSpell` and `CompleteCasting` increment `spells_cast` only; `BreakConcentration` and `BeginConcentration` always increment `operations` (FR-133).

| Use case | Span suffix | `result` values | Counter | Extra attributes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `CastSpell` | `cast_spell` | `cast`, `started`, `rejected` | `spells_cast` | `cast_source`, `srd5e.class`, `rejection` |
| `CompleteCasting` | `complete_casting` | `cast`, `rejected` | `spells_cast` | `cast_source`, `srd5e.class`, `rejection` |
| `BreakConcentration` | `break_concentration` | `ended`, `none`, `casting_failed` | `operations` | `concentration_reason` |
| `BeginConcentration` | `begin_concentration` | `began`, `casting_failed` | `operations` | none |

Attributes (all low cardinality). `srd5e.character.rejection` (rejections only) is the snake_case of the *first* reason in the fixed order (FR-080) from the kinds of section 5 (`MissingComponent(verbal)` becomes `missing_component_verbal`), including `casting_not_in_progress` and `no_slot_available`. `srd5e.character.cast_source`: `slot`, `cantrip`, `ritual`, `feature_granted`, `scroll`, `duplicated` (on `cast_spell`, `complete_casting`, `spells_cast`). `srd5e.character.concentration_reason` (`break_concentration`): `another_effect`, `incapacitated`, `dead`, `ended`, `failed_save`. `srd5e.class` on cast spans and counters (absent for a scroll cast, FR-143). `spell_slot_level` is `spellcasting.md`'s histogram; `CastSpell` and `CompleteCasting` also record it (not for a cantrip, Ritual, scroll, duplicated or feature-granted cast).

| Signal | Type | Unit | Attributes | Emitted by | Purpose | Suggested monitoring |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `srd5e.character.<operation>` spans | Span | n/a | as above | each use case | Follow one call, including its duration | Error status is a caller bug; a rejection is not an error |
| `srd5e.character.spells_cast` | Counter | `{cast}` | `srd5e.outcome`, `result` (`cast`, `started`, `rejected`), `cast_source`, `srd5e.class`, `rejection` (only when `rejected`) | `CastSpell`, `CompleteCasting` | Casting throughput and illegal-cast attempts | Alert on a sustained rise in the `rejected` share; compare `started` with `casting_failed` |
| `srd5e.character.operations` | Counter | `{operation}` | `operation`, `srd5e.outcome`, `result` | `BreakConcentration`, `BeginConcentration` | Owned by character-aggregate.md; this spec adds operation values | Watch `casting_failed` against `spells_cast` `started` |
| `srd5e.character.spell_cast_resolved` | Log event, `FINE` | n/a | `srd5e.outcome`, `srd5e.character.result` | `CastSpell` | One debug summary per cast | None; debug only |

These entries feed `doc/observability/signals.md`.

## 9. Open questions and decisions
