## 1. Purpose and scope

The general rules of SRD 5.2.1 for magic items, as a model that rules code can ask questions of: category defaults, rarity and value, identification, Attunement, wearing and wielding, activation, charges and recharging ("The Next Dawn"), curses, durability, crafting, and sentient items and their conflict with the bearer. Potion Miscibility is included because it is a general Potions rule (p. 204). Phase 3 delivers the types and rules; the crafting requirements (FR-074 to FR-087, FR-121) depend on `equipment-rules.md` and their bundled-data scenarios wait for phase 5 (see [../roadmap.md](../roadmap.md)).

This spec owns **behaviour and state** (Attunement, charges, wearing, crafting requirements, sentient traits) and nothing else (shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-058: it is a `character` spec). The closed vocabulary and static tables these rules read (categories and defaults, rarity values and crafting rows, tools, resilience facts, Potion Miscibility, attunement facts, sentient tables, `MagicItemFacts`) are `content` types owned by `content-magic-items.md` section 4.8 (FR-078 to FR-097), which also owns the catalogue ("Magic Items A-Z", pp. 209-253). This spec reuses them and defines none; the moved requirement ids are in section 4.11.

**Referenced, not depended on** (the host passes their results in): `spellcasting.md` (`SpellSaveDC`, `SpellAttackBonus`, used by this spec's FR-027), `class-rogue.md` (FR-095, the Attunement limit), `magical-effects.md`, `conditions.md` (Charmed) and `architecture-guardrails.md` (FR-097, for FR-123). The equipment and spell types used (`Cost`, `Tool`, `EquipmentEntry`, `SettlementSize`, `SpellLevel`) reach this spec through `content-magic-items.md` and `equipment-rules.md`.

**Supersedes** (shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-048; this is the topic-specific spec): the former attuned-set requirement of `magical-effects.md`, withdrawn there in favour of FR-054 and FR-117 here; `magical-effects.md` keeps `CreatureReturnedToLife` (its FR-110). The deferrals in `content-equipment.md`, `equipment-rules.md` and `content-magic-items.md` are pointers, not requirements, so nothing there is withdrawn.

**Out of scope:**

- Vocabulary and static tables (including the attunement numbers three items, 100 feet, 24 hours): `content-magic-items.md`. This spec reads them and does not state them.
- The catalogue entries, their text, rarity and prerequisites as data, and the Spell Scroll and Potion of Healing gear rows (`content-magic-items.md` FR-003). Rules here take an item's facts as values.
- What a particular item does: effects, a curse's effect, charge cost per property, and how an Artifact is destroyed (p. 206) are item text kept verbatim as content.
- Game Master guidance: what a settlement sells (p. 205), magical clues and experiments (p. 102), GM control of sentient items (p. 207), anatomy fit (p. 205), when a conflict "can arise" (p. 208). Where a rule lets the GM decide, the model takes the decision as a command value (Q16 in FR-065, FR-069, FR-070, FR-082) and never decides.
- Scribing Spell Scrolls and brewing Potions of Healing (p. 103): `equipment-rules.md` (`Crafting.RequirementsFor`, FR-040 to FR-043, FR-049 to FR-051); FR-075 only hands over (character-side prerequisites for a Spell Scroll and a Potion of Healing: `tool-proficiency-and-crafting.md`).
- The text and casting of Identify, Remove Curse and Greater Restoration (`content-spells.md`); only their effect on Attunement (FR-059, FR-118) and knowledge (FR-032, FR-063) is here.
- Time of day, map distance and the clock: dawn, "24 hours" and "100 feet" arrive as host values (FR-033, FR-053).
- Properties that recharge without charges (p. 206): item text and host state (Q13). Holding a weapon and strapping a Shield (p. 103) are use, not slots; a Shield takes no `WornSlot` (FR-066).
- Display text and persistence.
- **Not in the PDF:** nothing core was found missing on pp. 204-208 against pp. 102-103. Two things the rules refer to are not defined and are not invented: how fractional crafting days round (FR-083) and what happens to a cursed item's curse after Greater Restoration ends its owner's Attunement (FR-118, Q17).

## 5. Model

Go naming: enum members are exported typed constants of their enum type (a member written in lower case in a table or requirement, such as `endAttunement`, `curseIntact` or `gmAllows`, is the constant with an upper-case first letter, qualified by the type name where unqualified names would collide); lower-camel names used as fields or accessors (such as `isCompleted`) are exported struct fields or methods named with an upper-case first letter (`IsCompleted`), and as a method on a type that also needs a same-named field the method form wins and the field stays unexported; sets are deduplicated slices in a documented order; parameters stay lower camel case. A stateless rules service written `XRules.Method` is a zero-size struct `XRules` with value-receiver methods, usable as its zero value; consumers that want to replace one declare their own one-method interface.

**Context split.** Everything defined here is `character` (behaviour and state). The `content` vocabulary and tables it reads (section 3, "Reused") carry SRD facts only; `character` owns every type that interprets them or holds state, including the derived rules over `content` values (`ChargeRecoveryRules`, `MagicItemResilienceRules.Effective`, `MagicItemFactResolution`, `MagicItemValues`, `IncorporatedItemCost`, `AttunementPrerequisiteReader`). `content` never imports `character`, so no edge points back (`architecture.md` section 2). `MagicItemEntry` carries no structured facts; `MagicItemFacts` (`content-magic-items.md` FR-091) is the host-supplied contract for them. There is no aggregate and no `MagicItem` entity: the host stores attunement and charges as values (Q11).

**Value objects** (immutable, validated in the constructor): `AttunementLimit(int)` of at least 3 (FR-049); `MagicItemInstanceID` and `PairID`, non-blank trimmed strings (as `CharacterID`, `character-aggregate.md` FR-001, FR-002); `AttunableItem`, `AttunedItem`, `ShortRestFocus` (FR-114), `AttuneCandidate(classes, spellcasting)` with a copied set of `ClassID`s; `ItemCharges` (FR-029), `ChargeKnowledge`, `SpellFromItem`, `WorkHours`, `CraftingTeam(crafter, assistants)`, `CrafterQualification(member, arcana, tools, preparedSpellsEachDay)`, `AssistantAllowance`, `ResolvedRarity`, `RarityChoice`, `SentientTraits`, `SpecialPurposeSelection`, `SentientAbilityRoll`, `SpecialPurpose` variants, `DescribedTarget`, `SentientDemands`, `SentientControlEffect`, `SentientSave`.

**Domain services** (pure, stateless): `MagicItemValues`, `IncorporatedItemCost`, `MagicItemFactResolution`, `MagicItemResilienceRules`, `ChargeRecoveryRules`, `AttunementEligibility`, `AttunementEnding`, `MagicalPropertiesAccess`, `PairedItemBenefit`, `CommandWord`, `ScrollReading`, `AmmunitionMagic`, `BaseItemSelection`, `ItemSpellcastingAbility`, `ChargeKnowledge`, `CraftingMaterials`, `MagicItemCrafting`, `ResolvedRarity`, `AttunementPrerequisiteReader`, `SentientConflict`.

**Failures.** Expected rule outcomes are results (`architecture.md` section 6): every rejection named in section 4 (`AttuneToMagicItemRejected`, `EndMagicItemAttunementRejected`, `WornItemsSlotOccupied`, `MagicItemInsufficientCharges`, `RechargeMagicItemNotDue`, `ShortRestFocusRejected`, `RarityNotResolved`, `SentientTraitsRejected` and the like) is a typed value with data, not text. `MagicItemNotCraftable` is an outcome inside `PlanMagicItemCraftingPlanned` (FR-087), not a rejection. Untrusted values (a limit below 3, a blank id, a Neutral Aligned item built directly, a roll, a count, a demands list, a rolled alignment and purpose) are rejected by `NewX` / `ParseX` / `ForRoll`, which return `(T, error)` where the error is the typed rejection (matching `srderr.ErrInvalidInput`, readable with `errors.As`); `MustX` helpers panic and are for literals only. Infrastructure failure is a typed `*srderr.PortError` from a port, never caught here.

**`sentientSaveRoll`** (FR-123) is the one unexported helper type in package `character` (file `sentient_save_roll.go`): constructed with a `dice.Roller` by each of the three sentient use cases, one method `roll`, no exported identifier (so it is not classified as a use case and the "no use case mentions another use case" scan is not tripped), composing the pure `d20` resolver, containing no rule, recording no signal (the calling use case records its own, shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-044).

## 7. Observability

Domain services and value objects are **silent by design**: they are pure and return what happened in values and events. The use cases are instrumented (`doc/rules/observability.md` section 2).

1. **What can go wrong?** A `dice.Roller` failure or wrong-expression roll; a `ContentSource` failure or missing magic item, spell, incorporated item, tool set or scribing table; rule rejections (attunement, end of attunement, recharge not due, too few potions, crafting shortfall, unresolved rarity, sentient power spent); programmer errors.
2. **How would an operator know?** A returned port failure: span error status and `error.type` (`dice_roller_error`, `content_error`, shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-041). A returned failure: `srd5e.outcome=failure` with the variant in `srd5e.character.result` and, for a rejection, `srd5e.character.rejection`. A rule outcome sets no error status; a returned content failure (`content_missing`, `content_incomplete`) sets error status on the span; neither carries `error.type` or a log (`errors-and-results.md` FR-043, `character-aggregate.md` FR-072). Programmer errors (a `MustX` panic) are not errors and are never recorded as `error.type`.
3. **What is needed to diagnose?** Operation, outcome, result, rejection or error type, and for crafting the shortfall kinds; ids and totals are in the returned result, not attributes.
4. **What is counted or timed?** Calls, outcomes and duration per operation through the existing `srd5e.character.operations` counter and `srd5e.character.duration` histogram (`character-aggregate.md`), which gain the operation values below. No new instrument.
5. **Is existing instrumentation still correct?** Yes. `srd5e.character.result` is not a closed set (`character-aggregate.md` FR-088); this spec adds `not_due`, `content_incomplete`, `resisted`, `unavailable`, `demands_made`, `planned`, `generated` and `unchanged` without changing existing values. `plan_magic_item_crafting` uses the child span `srd5e.character.load_content` as "any `character` use case that looks up content"; the narrower "child of `create` and `level_up`" wording of that row is a wording fix requested in Q27.
6. **What should a consumer monitor?** Any error status or `error.type`; a rising `rejected` share for `attune_magic_item`; any `content_missing` or `content_incomplete` for `plan_magic_item_crafting`.
7. **Is anything now noisy?** No. No log is emitted: a rule outcome is an attribute and a failure is recorded once on span and counter.

The sentient use cases roll the save through the pure `d20` functions and record only their own signals (shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-044), taking `srd5e.d20_test.kind` from the domain result; `srd5e.d20_test.resolved` and the other `ResolveD20Test` signals do not fire for them. `srd5e.d20_test.kind` is a span attribute of the three sentient spans only, and `srd5e.character.recharge_moment` of `recharge_magic_item` only.

Result values: `AttuneToMagicItemApplied`, `EndMagicItemAttunementApplied`, `RechargeMagicItemApplied`, `CheckCraftingMaterialsAvailable`, `AttemptSentientItemControlTaken` and `RepeatSentientControlSaveEnded` are `changed`; `RollPotionMiscibilityRolled` `rolled`; `PlanMagicItemCraftingPlanned` `planned`; `GenerateSentientItemTraitsGenerated` `generated`; the two `...Resisted` variants `resisted`; `ResolveSentientItemConflictDemandsMade` `demands_made`; `RepeatSentientControlSaveContinues` `unchanged`; `RechargeMagicItemNotDue` `not_due`; `CheckCraftingMaterialsUnavailable` `unavailable`; every `...Rejected` (and `PlanMagicItemCraftingRarityUnresolved`) `rejected` or `rarity_unresolved` per its row; the two content failures `content_missing` and `content_incomplete`.

`srd5e.outcome` is `success` or `failure` only: `success` for applied, rolled, generated, planned (including `MagicItemNotCraftable`), resisted, demands-made, taken, ended, continues or unavailable; `failure` for a rejection, `not_due`, `content_missing`, `content_incomplete` and an unresolved rarity.

Shared signals (owned by `character-aggregate.md`): the counter `srd5e.character.operations`, whose `srd5e.character.operation` gains `attune_magic_item`, `end_magic_item_attunement`, `recharge_magic_item`, `mix_potions`, `check_crafting_materials`, `plan_magic_item_crafting`, `generate_sentient_traits`, `resolve_sentient_conflict`, `attempt_sentient_control`, `repeat_sentient_control_save` (with `srd5e.outcome`, `srd5e.character.result`, `srd5e.character.rejection`, `error.type` as in the spans below), and the histogram `srd5e.character.duration` (p99 regression for `plan_magic_item_crafting` only; the others are in-memory).

## 9. Open questions and decisions

All questions are settled; none is escalated. Settled in a requirement or another spec: Q1 (shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-035, FR-058), Q2 FR-075, Q3 FR-114, Q7 FR-083, Q9 FR-085, Q12 FR-038, Q14 (shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-009), Q15 FR-051, Q16 FR-065, FR-069, FR-070, FR-082, Q18 (shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-053), Q19 (`dice-and-rolls.md` already cites pp. 200 and 215 correctly), Q20 (not this spec's), Q21 FR-117, Q22 FR-066, Q23 (table "Aligned purpose"), Q24 FR-116, Q25 FR-122, Q26 FR-123, Q28 FR-056, Q29 FR-017 and FR-084.

Settled, one line each (the SRD is the authority; web research only informed the readings marked "interpretation"):

- **Q4** (`Charmed(charmer)` holds a `CombatantID`; a sentient item is not a combatant): FR-101 and FR-113 return upstream facts only and the host applies Charmed. Design basis: `character` cannot depend on `combat` (shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-013). Amendment requested for `conditions.md`: a closed `Charmer` (`CharmedByCombatant`, `CharmedByItem(MagicItemInstanceID)` or an opaque id).
- **Q5** (resilience to damage handling): interpretation. p. 206 gives most items "Resistance to all damage" and an Artifact is "impervious to damage" unless destroyed its special way; the plain reading is that no damage affects it, so `combat` maps `resistantToAllDamage` to Resistance to every damage type, `nonmagicalDurability` to none and `impervious` to Immunity to all damage. Amendment requested for `hit-points-and-damage.md`.
- **Q6** (`ActivationTime` against `StandardAction` / `PaladinActionKind`): package design; `ActivationTime` stays a `content` fact (`content-magic-items.md` FR-079) and `combat` maps `MagicAction` to the Magic action and `BonusAction` to a bonus-action grant (SRD 5.2.1, "Activating a Magic Item", p. 206). Amendments requested for `initiative-and-actions.md` and `class-paladin.md` (drop the claim that `ActionKind` is used here).
- **Q8** (Plate Armor "craft it" path, 150 days beside 50 days): interpretation; p. 207 says only "pay that entire cost or craft that item" and gives no rule combining the times, so FR-084 reports both requirements, sums nothing, and the host schedules them.
- **Q10** (lowest caster level; Concentration when bypassing): interpretation; p. 206 casts the spell "at the lowest possible spell and caster level", so `ItemCasterLevel.Lowest` means the spell's lowest level and the base (unscaled) cantrip tier, which `spellcasting.md` applies; the user concentrates "if the spell requires" it, so a bypass is not a cast and sets nothing (FR-026). Amendment requested for `spellcasting.md`.
- **Q11** (where `AttunementSet` lives): the host keeps it beside the `Character` (no inventory aggregate, section 5); `AttunementSet.IsAttunedTo(instanceID)` feeds the Warlock `attunedByAnother` fact.
- **Q13** (recharge dice text, recharge without charges, item-specific destruction): item text kept verbatim as content, not modelled (section 1); `regainedFor` caps at the expended count (FR-034).
- **Q17** (re-attuning after Greater Restoration): neither spell nor "Cursed Items" (p. 206) forbids a new attempt, so FR-040 to FR-051 apply unchanged; `CurseStatus` is unchanged (FR-118) and any item-specific consequence is item text.
- **Q27** (`character-aggregate.md` `load_content` span parent): wording fix requested there; nothing here depends on it.
