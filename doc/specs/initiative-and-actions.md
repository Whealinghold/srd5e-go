## 1. Purpose and scope
How a combat is ordered and how a combatant spends its turn: rolling Initiative, fixing the order (including ties), stepping through rounds and turns, the action economy (an action, a Bonus Action, a Reaction, one free interaction with an object) and movement (Speed, special speeds, Difficult Terrain, other creatures' spaces, Dropping Prone). The unit of state is the `TurnTracker` aggregate; every operation takes the state and returns the new state plus the events it produced (`doc/rules/architecture.md` §4). The engine models no map: the host says what a move passes through (terrain, occupants, whether it ends in an occupied space) and the engine charges the movement and judges legality.
**Ownership** (`shared-types-and-ownership.md` is binding and wins over this spec).
- Owned here: `InitiativeCount`, `TurnOrder`, `TurnTracker` (order, round, active combatant, per-turn resources, action economy), the use case `AdvanceTrackerTurn`, the entry type `InitiativeStanding` and the events `TrackerRoundStarted`, `TrackerTurnStarted`, `TrackerTurnEnded`; the use cases `SwapInitiative`, `GrantAdditionalAction`, `RecordFeatUse` (FR-155 to FR-157); `BonusActionSource` (FR-158); `ReadiedSpell` and `HeldSpellInput`, moved down from `readying-a-spell.md` (rank 26 cannot be imported, FR-163); these names keep every name declared once in the package (shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-021; `content-monsters.md` keeps `InitiativeEntry`).
- Imported, not defined: `CombatantID` (built by `NewCombatantID`, combat-foundations.md FR-005), `CombatEvent`, `CombatOperation`, `CombatResultClass`, `CombatTelemetry`, `CombatSignals` from `combat-foundations.md` (shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-012, FR-015); `CreatureSize` and `FeatureID`, `FeatID`, `ItemID`, `SpellID` from package `content` (shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-005; content-schema-and-decoder.md FR-109, FR-114), `ActivationTime` from `content-magic-items.md` (FR-079), and `InitiativeSwap` and `FeatUsageLimit` from `feats-behaviour.md` (`character` is upstream of `combat`, architecture.md §2); `Modifier`, `RollMode`, `RollCircumstance` from the kernel packages `modifier` and `dice`; `Roll` from `d20` (shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-002; d20-tests.md FR-055), so a reroll or replacement can act on the Initiative roll; `RollContributions`, `ConditionActionAvailability`, `ProneMovement` from `conditions.md`; `D20TestSignals` (the shared `srd5e.d20_test.roll_mode` key, resolve-d20-test-use-case.md FR-040); `dice.Roller`, `dice.Expression`, `dice.Roll`, a `*srderr.PortError` for `dice.Port` from `dice-and-rolls.md` (doubles from the `<pkg>test` packages). `d20.Resolver.Resolve` is not used.
- Consumers (not dependencies, shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-015): `encounter-aggregate.md` holds a `TurnTracker`, reads its round and active combatant and wraps the tracker events into its own; `combat-action-resolution.md` is the only caller of both `conditions.md` and `attacks.md` (shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-026) and passes results in as the inputs declared here.
- Results and events follow shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-017 to FR-019 and combat-foundations.md FR-015 to FR-018: each use case has its own closed `<UseCase>Result` with operation-prefixed variants, and the events form the one closed sub-family `TurnEvent` (FR-125, FR-126, FR-128). `CombatEvent` is an interface with an unexported marker method, and each closed family is a sub-interface in the same package (combat-foundations.md D-1).
- No use case calls another (shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-044). `RollInitiative` composes `Roll` and `RollMode`; every other use case but `SwapInitiative` wraps one public `TurnTracker` method (FR-140 to FR-149, FR-162 excepted), the pure operations that `encounter-aggregate.md` and `combat-action-resolution.md` call directly.
- Attack and Help (shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-048): `attack-and-help-actions.md` owns their mechanics. This spec keeps only their `StandardAction` entries and the record that the action was taken (FR-050); a host needing the Help expiry calls `HelpBenefits.ExpireAtStartOfTurn` on `TrackerTurnStarted`.
- Telemetry goes through `CombatTelemetry` and the shared `srd5e.combat.*` signals (section 4.9).
**Out of scope:**
- Resolving what an action does: attack rolls and damage (`attacks.md`), spells cast with Magic, the checks of Hide, Influence, Search and Study, Utilize's object effects. This spec records that the action was taken and used the turn's action.
- The conditions themselves (`conditions.md`). They arrive as query results (`RollContributions` from `ConditionEffects.initiative`, `ConditionActionAvailability`, `ProneMovement`); the two effects named here (Prone from sharing a space, dropping Prone) are returned as events, not applied. The Exhaustion roll reduction is the caller's to pass (FR-111). The host maps `ConditionEffects.speed` onto `MovementSpeeds` and reports it through `UpdateMovementSpeeds` (FR-116); the engine never reads `EffectiveSpeed` (FR-117).
- The extra cost of dragging a Grappled creature: the host takes it from grappling.md FR-062 (`ExtraMovementCostPerFoot`, shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-029) and passes it as `ExtraCostPerFoot` (FR-077). Rerolling or replacing an Initiative die (the roll is exposed as a `Roll`, FR-002, FR-139). Class features, spells and items that grant Bonus Actions, Reactions, extra actions or Initiative bonuses; only the plug-in interface (`BonusActionGrant`, `FeatureActionChoice`, `FeatureReaction`, `SpellReaction`, `GrantedAction`, `AdditionalActionGrant`; FR-112 to FR-114, FR-151, FR-155, FR-158, FR-161) is here. A grant's own timing ("unless the Bonus Action's timing is specified", p. 10) is the content's concern.
- Readying a spell ("Ready [Action]", p. 187): `readying-a-spell.md` FR-001 to FR-012 (shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-048; it builds on FR-038, FR-056, FR-072 and FR-073 here) and `spellcasting.md` (Concentration). Follow-up F-1 (declined): section 9. Position, reach geometry and the grid ("Playing on a Grid", p. 13; `grid-positions-and-movement.md`, prose only, no dependency). The engine works in feet; the grid's 1- and 2-square costs equal the 1- and 2-feet-per-foot costs of FR-077. Mounted Combat (pp. 15-16) and Underwater Combat (p. 16); Jump distances, whether a flier falls and burrowing limits (`movement-lifting-and-flight.md` FR-007 to FR-021, FR-036 to FR-052), and Falling distances (`environment-hazards-and-objects.md`); Communicating (free, p. 13) and Doing Nothing beyond that it is allowed (FR-041). Ending Combat (p. 14). Adding, removing or repositioning combatants mid-combat: `combatant-roster-changes.md` owns the rules and the encounter-level operations; this spec only hosts the pure tracker operations (FR-162).
- Monster stat blocks' own Bonus Actions, Reactions and Legendary Actions (p. 254 onward): the stat-block entries and their use belong to `monster-stat-block-actions.md`, their timing and uses to `monster-limited-use.md` (shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-060), which builds on `TrackerTurnEnded`. A `FeatureID` may name a stat-block part (`monster-stat-block-actions.md` FR-005); this spec treats it as any feature id. A surprise round (the SRD defines Surprise only as Disadvantage on the Initiative roll, p. 13, p. 189) and a "delay your turn" action (not in SRD 5.2.1; the SRD suggests Ready "to delay acting", p. 14). Display text: the engine returns structured data only.

## 3. Vocabulary
| SRD term | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Initiative / Initiative count | `InitiativeCount` (over `int`) | "A combatant's check total is called their Initiative count, or Initiative for short" (p. 13). Any integer. The roll is a `Roll` (FR-002). |
| Initiative modifier | `Modifier` | Typically Dexterity modifier plus any additional modifier (p. 254); the input is the whole modifier. |
| Initiative score | `InitiativeCount` | "10 plus your Dexterity modifier" (p. 184) with the same additions (p. 254), +5 with Advantage, -5 with Disadvantage; used instead of a roll when the GM so chooses. `InitiativeMethod`: `rolled`, `score`. |
| Surprise / surprised | `Surprise` (`surprised`, `NotSurprised`) | Disadvantage on the Initiative roll (p. 13, p. 189); not a condition, so owned here. |
| roll-only modifier | `Modifier` | Applied to the rolled count only, never the score; carries the Exhaustion reduction (p. 181). |
| Initiative order | `TurnOrder` | Highest to lowest (p. 13). The type keeps the non-SRD name `TurnOrder` (section 9). `TiedGroup`: two or more combatants with equal counts. |
| round, turn | `TurnTracker.round`, `.ActiveCombatant` | A round is about 6 seconds (descriptive, not modelled). `TurnTracker` is not an SRD term: it holds the order, round and active turn. |
| action | `ActionChoice` (closed) | FR-150; "Player characters and monsters can also do things not covered by these actions" (p. 10). |
| the twelve actions | `StandardAction`, `SimpleStandardAction` | The Actions table (pp. 9-10); `SimpleStandardAction` is the ten other than Dash and Ready (FR-044). |
| Bonus Action | `BonusActionGrant`, `BonusActionEntitlement` | "You have a Bonus Action to take only if a rule explicitly says so" (p. 177). The grant names its source (`BonusActionSource`: a feature or an item, FR-158) and its `GrantedAction` (FR-151); an item's `ActivationTime` maps to it (FR-159). |
| Reaction, Opportunity Attack | `ReactionChoice`, `OpportunityAttackReaction`, `SpellReaction` (FR-161) | "The most common type of Reaction" (p. 10, p. 186). `DepartureMeans`: `action`, `BonusAction`, `reaction`, `speed` (p. 185). `Visibility`: `seen`, `unseen`. |
| Ready, trigger | `ReadiedAction`, `ReadyTriggerID`, `ReadiedResponse`, `HeldSpellInput` (FR-163) | The trigger is a host id with no description text (FR-137). |
| Speed; special speeds | `MovementSpeeds.walk`; `.burrow`, `.climb`, `.fly`, `.swim` | Feet on a turn (p. 188); special speeds optional. `MovementMode`: `walk`, `burrow`, `climb`, `crawl`, `fly`, `swim`. |
| Difficult Terrain | `Terrain.difficult` / `.open` | "Isn't cumulative" (p. 181). |
| occupied by another creature | `SegmentEnd` (`OccupiedByCreature`, `free`); `OccupantRelation` (`ally`, `other`) | p. 14. A space filled by objects (p. 185) is not modelled (section 9). |
| Incapacitated | `ConditionActionAvailability` (`available`, `none`) | "You can't take any action, Bonus Action, or Reaction" (p. 184); the refusal reason keeps the name `incapacitated`. |
| (package types, not SRD terms) | `TurnEvent`, `TurnRejection`, `TrackerStep` | Closed event family (FR-128); refusal reason held by `<UseCase>Rejected` (FR-126); result of each tracker operation (FR-140). |

Terms use SRD 5.2.1 wording exactly (`doc/rules/architecture.md` §3).

## 5. Model

Go naming: enum members are exported typed constants of their enum type (a member written in lower case in a table, such as `heavily`, is the constant with an upper-case first letter, qualified by the type name where unqualified names would collide); sets are deduplicated slices in a documented order; accessors are methods named after the field (`Position()`); parameters stay lower camel case. A stateless rules service written `XRules.Method` is a zero-size struct `XRules` with value-receiver methods, usable as its zero value; consumers that want to replace one declare their own one-method interface.
- **Value objects** (immutable, by value; validation point named):
  - `ReadyTriggerID` (defined type over `string`; `ParseReadyTriggerID` returns `(ReadyTriggerID, error)`, a `*ReadyTriggerIDError` for the empty or whitespace-only string; FR-137). Imported, not defined: `CombatantID`, `FeatureID`, `CreatureSize`, `ConditionActionAvailability`, `RollContributions`, `ProneMovement`, `Roll`. `InitiativeCount` (any integer, FR-011), `InitiativeMethod`, `Surprise`; `InitiativeParticipant` (`ids` at least one and unique, Initiative `Modifier`, roll-only `Modifier` default 0, `Surprise`, `RollContributions`, other `RollCircumstance`s; FR-014); closed `InitiativeBreakdown` (`RolledInitiative`, `ScoredInitiative`, FR-012); `InitiativeCount.FromRoll` (FR-139). `InitiativeStanding` (id, count), `TiedGroup`, `TieBreak` (FR-153), `TieBreakRejection` (ids and reason `UnknownID`, `RepeatedID`, `NotTied`, `SpansGroups`, `Partial`, `DuplicateGroup`), `TurnOrder` (ordered, complete, defensively copied, FR-026).
  - `MovementSpeeds` (`walk`, optional `burrow`, `climb`, `fly`, `swim`; `AdjustedBy`, `halved`, `doubled`; FR-075, FR-076, FR-109, FR-160), `SpeedName`, `MovementMode`, `SpeedUse` (`WalkingSpeed`, `SpecialSpeed`), `Terrain`, `SegmentEnd`, `OccupantRelation`, `Occupant` (`CreatureSize`, relation, `ConditionActionAvailability`), `MoveSegment` (distance above 0, mode, `SpeedUse`, terrain, occupants, end, `ExtraCostPerFoot`; FR-087), `CombatantProfile` (id, size, speeds). `StandardAction`, `SimpleStandardAction` (FR-044); `ActionChoice` and `GrantedAction` (FR-150, FR-151); `BonusActionGrant`, `BonusActionEntitlement`; `ReactionChoice` (`OpportunityAttackReaction(provoker, DepartureMeans, Visibility)`, `ReadiedActionReaction(ReadyTriggerID)`, `FeatureReaction(FeatureID, GrantedAction)`), `AttackTiming`; `ReadiedAction` (trigger, response, `HeldSpellInput`), `ReadiedResponse`, `ReadiedSpell`, `HeldSpellInput`; `AdditionalActionGrant`, `AdditionalActionScope`; `BonusActionSource`; `AllyConsent`; `PostureChange` (`DropProneChange`, `StandUpChange(ProneMovement)`), `DodgeBenefits`.
- **Aggregate and root:** `TurnTracker`. It holds the `TurnOrder`, the round, the active combatant, the active combatant's `TurnResources` (`MovedFeet`, the Dash extra, a `ResourceState` of `available` or `spent` for the action, Bonus Action and free interaction, and a `DisengageState`), the combatants whose Reaction is spent, those dodging, the readied actions, the profiles, the active turn's `AdditionalActionGrant` and its use, and the turn-limited feat uses (FR-155, FR-157). All of it compares by value (FR-154). The movement left on a speed is derived (FR-115), never stored. Its public operations (FR-141 to FR-148) return a `TrackerStep` and are what the use cases wrap (FR-149) and the `Encounter` calls; plus the query `DodgeBenefits` (FR-055). Construction is restricted by FR-123.
- **Events:** the 16 of FR-125 in `TurnEvent`; fields in FR-152.
- **Domain services** (stateless): `InitiativeOrdering` (sort, tied groups, tie-breaks, `establish`), `InitiativeScoring`, `MovementCost` (segment cost, governing speed, pass-through, terrain from occupants), `PostureRules`, `DodgeRules`.
- **Results (FR-126):** `RollInitiativeRolled(breakdowns)`; `InitiativeOrderEstablished(tracker, events)` | `InitiativeTiesUnresolved(groups)` | `InitiativeTieBreakRejected(rejections)`; `AdvanceTrackerTurnApplied` | `AdvanceTrackerTurnRejected(TurnRejection)`; `<UseCase>Applied(tracker, events)` | `<UseCase>Rejected(TurnRejection)` for the rest. `TurnRejection` is a closed variant set with one struct type per reason, named `Turn<Reason>Rejection` (`TurnUnknownCombatantRejection(id)`, `TurnIncapacitatedRejection`, `TurnNotActiveCombatantRejection`, `TurnActionAlreadyTakenRejection`, `TurnNoBonusActionGrantedRejection`, `TurnBonusActionAlreadyTakenRejection`, `TurnReactionAlreadyTakenRejection`, `TurnProvokerDisengagedRejection`, `TurnProvokerNotSeenRejection`, `TurnNoMatchingReadiedActionRejection(trigger)`, `TurnNeedsUtilizeActionRejection`, `TurnSpeedUnavailableRejection(speed)`, `TurnNoMovementRemainingRejection(SegmentIndex, MovementLeft)`, `TurnInsufficientMovementRejection(SegmentIndex, shortfall)`, `TurnBlockedByCreatureRejection(SegmentIndex)`, `TurnEndsInOccupiedSpaceRejection(SegmentIndex)`, `TurnSpeedIsZeroRejection`, `TurnAdditionalActionAlreadyGrantedRejection`, `TurnFeatUseLimitedRejection(feat)`, `TurnAllyNotWillingRejection`); `srd5e.combat.result` is the reason in snake_case (FR-100). Prose "refused with `reason`" means the matching rejection.
- **Invariants:** speeds 0 or more: `MovementSpeeds` constructor; a segment has positive distance: `MoveSegment`; a move has a segment: `MoveCommand` (FR-087, FR-133); a `TieBreak` names a combatant: its constructor; participant ids non-empty and unique: `InitiativeParticipant`, across entries: `RollInitiativeCommand`; counts unique and profiles one to one: `EstablishInitiativeOrderCommand`; a ranked, complete, tie-free order: a tracker exists only from `InitiativeOrderEstablished` and the roster operations keep it so (FR-123, FR-162); the one-action, Bonus Action, Reaction and interaction limits and Reaction regain: the tracker operations.
- **Failures:** results for every refusal (including `UnknownCombatant`, class `invalid`), `InitiativeTiesUnresolved`, `InitiativeTieBreakRejected`; no new `*srderr.PortError`, a `*srderr.PortError` for `dice.Port` propagates (FR-015); an invalid-input error (`srderr.ErrInvalidInput`) for malformed commands and values (FR-095).
- **Purity:** synchronous, deterministic, display-free; the dice reach the domain as a value.

## 6. Interface
Use cases are structs built by `NewX(roller or none, opts ...Option)` with one method `Run(ctx, cmd)`, taking an immutable `<UseCase>Command` (holding defensively copied slices, FR-133) and returning the closed `<UseCase>Result` of FR-126 (the `error` result is non-nil only for a port failure, FR-015, FR-054 of errors-and-results.md). All are synchronous and take `telemetry.Telemetry` through the `WithTelemetry` option, defaulting to the zero-value `telemetry.Noop` (shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-036). Every use case but `RollInitiative`, `EstablishInitiativeOrder` and `SwapInitiative` wraps one `TurnTracker` operation (FR-149).

- **Why these ports:** the only collaborator the engine does not control is the dice. Initiative is a D20 Test with no Difficulty Class, so `RollInitiative` does not use `d20.Resolver.Resolve`; it builds a `Roll` from the resolved `RollMode` and the faces (d20-tests.md FR-055) so a reroll or replacement can act on it (shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-002), and the count is a pure function of the roll and two modifiers (FR-139). **Port failure contract:** a `*srderr.PortError` for `dice.Port` is returned unchanged (FR-015); expected non-success is a result variant (FR-095, FR-126). **Rolling and establishing are separate** so a host can resolve ties after seeing the rolls without re-rolling (FR-019).
- **Exports:** package `combat` exports the use cases, commands, results, `TurnEvent` and events and the value objects above, not `CombatantID`, `CreatureSize`, `FeatureID`, `CombatEvent`, `CombatTelemetry` or `CombatSignals` (their owners export those). Domain services are not exported.

## 7. Observability
Answers to the seven questions (`doc/rules/observability.md` §2).
1. **What can go wrong?** The dice source fails (a `*srderr.PortError` for `dice.Port`, FR-015); a malformed command (an invalid-input error (`srderr.ErrInvalidInput`)); requests the rules forbid (normal rule outcomes); an unknown combatant or unusable tie-breaks (host input the engine could not use); unresolved ties (expected, host-fixable); a host that always sends refused requests is desynchronised from the tracker. 2. **How would an operator know?** Dice failure: span error status plus `srd5e.combat.errors` (combat-foundations.md FR-032). Invalid input (`unknown_combatant`, `tie_break_rejected`): `operations` with `result_class` `invalid` and error status, never in `errors` (errors-and-results.md FR-043; combat-foundations.md FR-031). Refusals and `InitiativeTiesUnresolved`: `result_class` `refused`, `failure` outcome, no error status. Invalid-input errors from constructors are returned to the caller and recorded on the span by the use case that received them.
3. **What is needed to diagnose?** The result itself (breakdown, refusal data, tied groups, rejections) and the shared low-cardinality attributes, plus method, roll mode, tie outcome and movement mode. Combatant ids are not attributes. 4. **What should be counted or timed?** Initiative counts and sizes, ties, movement spent. Turns, rounds and actions are `srd5e.combat.events` by kind (FR-103); requests by outcome and reason are `operations`; duration and errors are shared.
5. **Is existing instrumentation still correct?** Yes. Conventions are settled once for the context (combat-foundations.md FR-027 to FR-037; shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-041). When the `Encounter` calls the silent tracker operations (FR-149) each layer records once, so nothing is counted twice. 6. **What should a consumer monitor?** Sustained `errors` (dice source failing); a rising `invalid` share for one operation (bad ids or tie-breaks) or `refused` share for one result (desynchronised host); `initiative_ties` `unresolved` or `rejected` (FR-102); p99 `operation_duration`. 7. **Is anything now noisy?** No logs (a refusal is a normal rule outcome, observability.md §7); counters are per call, not per combatant, so no hot-loop volume.
The domain types are **silent by design** (observability.md §3); signals come from the use cases through `CombatTelemetry`. Shared rows are owned by combat-foundations.md (FR-037); this spec adds the closed sets they take: `srd5e.combat.operation` is one of the thirteen names of FR-132, `srd5e.combat.result` one of FR-100, `srd5e.combat.event` one of FR-129. Its own signals:

| Signal | Type | Attributes | Purpose | Suggested monitoring |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `srd5e.combat.initiative_counts` | counter, unit `{initiative_count}` | `srd5e.combat.initiative_method` (`rolled`, `score`), `srd5e.d20_test.roll_mode` (`normal`, `advantage`, `disadvantage`; shared key, `D20TestSignals`) | Throughput and roll-mode mix of counts produced, also for `score` | Sudden shifts in the Advantage and Disadvantage share |
| `srd5e.combat.initiative_participants` | histogram, unit `{combatant}` | `srd5e.combat.initiative_method` | Combatant ids per command (FR-101) | Runaway growth |

| `srd5e.combat.turn_movement_spent` | histogram, unit `[ft_i]` | `srd5e.combat.movement_mode` (`walk`, `burrow`, `climb`, `crawl`, `fly`, `swim`, `posture` for a stand-up) | Movement per accepted move, one sample per mode (FR-104) | Shift in the distribution |

## 9. Open questions and decisions
| Question | Recommendation | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Follow-up F-1 of `mounted-and-underwater-combat.md` (`SpendMovement`) | Not added: that spec's D-4 takes no `TurnTracker`. A new operation needs `NotActiveCombatant` and `InsufficientMovement` rules, a `MovementSpent` event and a histogram sample duplicating `move` and FR-091; it would be a ninth tracker operation, so it is declined; the caller charges the cost (that spec's D-4). | decided: not added (F-1 declined) |

**Settled (one line each, decision cited):** "two sizes larger or smaller" means a difference of at least two sizes (FR-080, 4.7d): p. 14 omits "at least" but an exact-two reading would let a Medium creature pass a Huge one yet be blocked by a Gargantuan one, and a size gap is a threshold, so a wider gap is no tighter (interpretation, confirmed by common table ruling). A climb or swim by a Climb or Swim Speed ignores the whole extra cost of "Climbing" (p. 178) and "Swimming" (p. 189), including its "2 extra feet in Difficult Terrain", because "this extra cost" refers to the sentence's full figure; such a foot costs 1 even on Difficult Terrain, and walking climbs pay 1 + 1 climbing + 1 Difficult Terrain (FR-077; interpretation). Dash with a switch of speed: the movement left on speed S is S plus the Dash extra minus the movement used (FR-084, FR-115), the only reading of "how much farther you can move" (p. 188) that keeps one shared tally; the SRD gives no combined example, so it is tested against the formula only. Roster changes mid-combat: the SRD gives no rule ("The Initiative order remains the same from round to round", p. 13) ; `combatant-roster-changes.md` decides them and the tracker hosts the pure operations (FR-162). Extra actions from features: the host grants one additional action for the turn (FR-155); a defeated or fled combatant that stays in the order still ends its turn with nothing used (FR-041). Prone crawling is not enforced by the tracker, which knows no conditions: the host supplies only crawl segments and `ChangePosture` stands up (p. 186); `conditions.md` (`ProneMovement`) owns the rule. "The other creature" in the shared-space rule means every creature sharing the space (FR-043, 4.3b): a combatant ends Prone unless Tiny or larger than all (interpretation: being larger than only one of several still leaves it sharing a space with an equal or larger creature, p. 14). A feature cannot grant Ready (FR-151): it needs a trigger, response and holding rule beyond the action's own Ready and no cited SRD text grants one; a content spec that finds one requests a `GrantsReady(ReadiedAction)` variant. ties inside a group of identical creatures are an ordinary tie settled by the host (FR-019; "the GM decides the order among tied monsters", p. 13). Halved Speeds round down per speed (FR-109; "Round Down", p. 187). `CreatureSize` is owned by `content` (shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-005; FR-092 here) and `CombatantID` by `combat-foundations.md` (shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-012). Initiative does not go through `d20.Resolver.Resolve` (no Difficulty Class) but keeps a `Roll` (shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-002; FR-139 here). Ignoring a readied trigger needs no command: the action expires (FR-038, FR-073). Forced movement and Teleportation do not provoke ("Opportunity Attacks", p. 15; "Teleportation", p. 190), so only the four listed means are a `DepartureMeans` (FR-068). Refusals are a closed `TurnRejection` with data, in a total order (FR-118, FR-126); `UnknownCombatant` is `invalid`, other refusals `refused` (combat-foundations.md FR-031; FR-100 here); a returned failure carries no `error.type` (errors-and-results.md FR-043). `CombatEvent` is not closed (combat-foundations.md D-1; shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-019). A space filled by objects is not modelled; `SegmentEnd.OccupiedByCreature` follows p. 14 and the host reports objects as it sees fit (FR-081). A granted Bonus Action Dash or Disengage works on the active combatant's own turn, a `FeatureReaction`'s grant is only recorded (FR-112 to FR-114, FR-134). A readied move is validated at the trigger, not at Ready (FR-057, FR-074). The Grappled extra cost is host-supplied (FR-077). `EstablishInitiativeOrderResult` lives in the tracker's files (FR-123). Attack and Help mechanics belong to `attack-and-help-actions.md` (shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-048). A readied action cannot be a Ready (FR-150). Closed grants and breakdowns replace nil values and flags (FR-012, FR-151). A supplied `ProneMovement` decides cost and refusal, the profile the movement left (FR-091, FR-117). An unknown id in `AdvanceTrackerTurn`'s updates is a refusal (FR-042, FR-119). The event is `BonusActionUsed` because `class-monk.md` owns `BonusActionTaken` (FR-063) and `FellProneInSharedSpace` is past tense (FR-125). Disengage stops an Opportunity Attack for every `DepartureMeans` (FR-069): "Avoiding Opportunity Attacks", p. 15, restricts it to no means and all four (p. 185) are ways of leaving reach (interpretation). The type keeps the name `TurnOrder` (not the SRD's "Initiative order") because `encounter-aggregate.md` and `combat-action-resolution.md` (FR-014) already cite it; renaming is a follow-up for the three specs together. Consumer amendments (4.11): `RenewFeatUses` is the host's call after `RollInitiative`, not this spec's (a use case may not call another, shared-types-and-ownership.md FR-044; FR-156); a `ProvokerExempt` refusal for Flyby is declined (the tracker knows no traits; the host does not report the Reaction, as it does not for any exemption it judges, `monster-traits.md` FR-073); the held-spell types move down rather than the rank rising (FR-163); doubling follows halving across all speeds (FR-160, interpretation, web consensus is split but p. 188 favours it).
