# Notice

## Scope

This notice covers **only** the fifth-edition game content bundled with this
module (rules data such as classes, species, feats, spells, equipment and
monster statistics), which lives under `content/data/`.

The bundled game content comes from SRD 5.2.1 and is limited to the Wizard
class (pp. 77 to 79) and its Evoker subclass (p. 82), the Barbarian class and its Path of the Berserker subclass (pp. 28 to 30), the Fighter class and its Champion subclass (pp. 47 to 49), the Monk class and its Warrior of the Open Hand subclass (pp. 49 to 52), the Rogue class and its Thief subclass (pp. 61 to 64), the Cleric class and its Life Domain subclass (pp. 36 to 40), the Druid class and its Circle of the Land subclass (pp. 41 to 46), the Bard class and its College of Lore subclass (pp. 31 to 35), the Sorcerer class and its Draconic Sorcery subclass (pp. 64 to 70), the Warlock class and its Fiend Patron subclass (pp. 70 to 76), the Paladin class and its Oath of Devotion subclass (pp. 53 to 57), the Ranger class and its Hunter subclass (pp. 57 to 61), the species Dragonborn, Dwarf, Elf and Human (pp. 84 to
86) and 73 spell descriptions (pp. 107 to 175).

The hand-written test oracles, `content/testdata/srd/classes/oracle.json` (class data) and
`content/data/testdata/srd/spells_levels_0_1/spells.json` and `content/data/testdata/srd/spells_levels_2_3/spells.json` and `content/data/testdata/srd/spells_levels_4_9/spells.json` (spell data), transcribe the same pages and are covered by
this notice as well.

It does **not** cover the module's source code. The code is licensed under
the MIT License; see [LICENSE](LICENSE), which covers the code.
